## 3. NOTES

### Sources and how they behaved
- **Museum-own pages** (Volk Field `.mil`, ahcw.org, kelchmuseum.org, mitchellgallery.org, eaa838.org, bongcenter.org, dekeslaytonmuseum.org) were all fetched **9 September 2026** and all show current hours — that is my open-in-2026 evidence for those eight sites.
- **Live FAA registry**, queried today, is the strongest currency evidence in this state: it independently confirms the AHCW DC-3 (N33632), the Mitchell Gallery Curtiss Pusher (N1911D), and **ten** Kelch airframes as registered to those institutions at those street addresses, with certificates running to 2028–2031.
- **Joe Baugher** carried the monument work. His 1963/1964/1969/1970/1971/1977/1978/1979/1980 files are live; the **1944, 1951, 1953, 1956, 1957 and 1967 index links on joebaugher.com are broken** (they point at the site root) and those pages 404 or return HTTP 520 — I read them via the Internet Archive 2024 snapshots instead. Flagging this because anyone repeating this sweep will hit the same wall.
- **Aerial Visuals** `Locator.php?Region=Wisconsin` returned ~90 Wisconsin locations and was the discovery engine. It is *not* independent enough to stand alone, and it failed currency tests repeatedly (below).

### Corrections made, with evidence
1. **Volk Field F-4C.** Aerial Visuals places EF-4C **63-7594** at the Hardwood Range gunnery target field. Baugher shows it went to Hardwood as a target in 8/1994 but was recovered and **was on display at the Wisconsin National Guard Museum in 2011**. AV's range entry is stale by ~15 years.
2. **Volk Field F-16.** Aerial Visuals gives **80-0487**. Baugher: 80-0487 went to AMARC as FG0138 on 8 April 1994 and was still on inventory in 2008. AV is wrong. I have therefore **not** recorded an F-16 at Volk Field at all, even though the museum's own page lists one — I have no defensible serial. Blank row omitted rather than a wrong one.
3. **F-16A 79-0368.** A visit report (aviationmuseum.eu) puts it at Volk Field; Baugher and Aerial Visuals both put it at **Madison/Truax**. Two-to-one and more specific, so it is filed under Truax.
4. **Volk Field F-15.** The museum's own page says **F-15C**. Baugher says 77-0159 is an **F-15B** on display with the Wisconsin ANG at Camp Douglas, and AV agrees (c/n 0380/B050, a B-model construction number). Recorded as F-15B; the museum's own page is wrong.
5. **Volk Field KC-97.** AV says KC-97G; the museum and Baugher both say the airframe was **converted to KC-97L**. Recorded as KC-97L with KC-97G in aliases.
6. **C-130A 57-0464.** Aerial Visuals lists this single airframe at **both** Camp Douglas and Sparta. Baugher settles it: "seen at Camp McCoy, WI in use as a loading trainer with wings removed." It is a Fort McCoy training aid, not a Volk Field display. AV's Camp Douglas entry is a duplicate.
7. **T-33A 53-4903.** Baugher says Dane County Airport, Madison; AV says VFW Post 8483, Madison. I took AV's (later, mapped) position and said so in the row.
8. **False markings** recorded, not silently transcribed: T-33A 52-9770 at Lowell wears "979392"; T-33A 53-5905 at Stoughton wears "3-0905"; F-4C 63-7704 at Milwaukee wears Sijan's 64-0751; P-38L 44-53236 at Superior wears 42-103993 "Marge". Bare forms are in `aliases`, the explanation in `description`.
9. **AH-1F 68-15133** at Footville is a composite — restored with the tail boom of 68-17101. Noted in the row.

### Production-block checks
Every USAF/USAAF serial recorded was checked against its block: 44-72989 (P-51D-25-NA, 44-72027/73028 ✓); 44-53236 (P-38L-5-LO, 44-53008/53327 ✓); 44-30444 (B-25J, 44-28711/31510 ✓); 63-7704 and 63-7594 (F-4C, 63-7407/7713 ✓); 64-0751 as a marking (F-4C, 64-0654/0928 ✓); 53-1358/53-1359 (F-86H-10-NH, 53-1298/1528 ✓); 56-2353 (Baugher's own block header reads 56-2354/2379 = TF-102A-45-CO, so 2353 is the last of the preceding TF-102A-41-CO batch — consistent with AV's TF-102A-41-CO ✓). No serial in the output falls outside its block.

### Deliberate blanks
- **No `year_built`** except where a sourced construction/first-flight/acceptance/delivery date or an FAA `MFR YEAR` exists. Fiscal-year serial prefixes were never used as years.
- **Travel Air 4000 N2709**: FAA `MFR YEAR` reads 1942, which is impossible for the type. Left blank rather than repeat it.
- **T-33A 53-5849 c/n**: Baugher 580-9250 vs AV 580-5290. Omitted entirely.
- **Deke Slayton Museum coordinates**: I have the street address (200 W Main St, Sparta) but no mapped airframe position, no imagery reading and no geocoder. Blank beats a made-up decimal.
- **EAA 838 Waco Taperwing registration**: none published by the chapter, none sourced elsewhere. Blank.
- **Fort McCoy AH-1 / UH-1**: AV lists them with no serials. Blank tail numbers.

- **EAA Aviation Museum, Oshkosh** — already fully recorded, per instruction. (Its Aerial Visuals dossier of 330+ airframes was read but nothing returned.)
- **Kenosha Military Museum** — **closed as a Wisconsin site.** Baugher's 1965 file, tracing OH-6A 66-12974: "To Kenosha Military Museum, Pleasant Prairie, Wisconsin in 1995. - **Wisconsin Automotive Museum, Hartford** — searched; **no sourced aircraft**. The Hartford airframes Aerial Visuals lists (L-39C N139WS; Air Cargo Carriers C-23C 85-25345) belong to the municipal airport and a freight operator, not the museum.
- **"Wisconsin Aviation Museum," Fond du Lac** — the only descriptive page I found is AI-generated slop that conflates it with Wisconsin Aviation Inc, the Watertown-based FBO. - **Hardwood ANG Air-to-Ground Gunnery Range, Nekoosa** (70+ airframes incl. EF-4C, A-4, A-6, F-8H, KC-135R 57-1470) — a live target range, not a display site.
