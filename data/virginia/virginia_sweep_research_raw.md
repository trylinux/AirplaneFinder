## NOTES

### Sources and their weight
- **Best currency evidence in this package**: the **FAA registry name search** on TSI Holdco Inc (the Yagen family holding entity behind the Military Aviation Museum) returned **82 live registrations**, and a spot check of N943HH returned owner TSI Holdco Inc, Virginia Beach, Hawker Siddeley Hurricane MkIIB, year 1943, s/n 56022, certificate valid to 2031. That is 2026-current.
- **Rod Bearden's 2017 photo log** and **militaryaircrafthistorian** independently agree on eight Air Power Park serials — two genuinely separate on-site readings.
- **HMdb interpretive markers** installed for Hampton's 2024 park refurbishment carry delivery dates and service histories written from USAF records; they are the source for F-105D 61-0073's 29 January 1962 delivery and RF-4C 69-0372's 19 August 1970 delivery.
- **Aerial Visuals** dossier for F-14D 164604 carries a 16 September 2022 photo in place at Oceana — the freshest airframe-level evidence I obtained on that site.
- **Official base publishing**: the JBLE article on the Langley F-4C is a primary source on both identity and location.

### Compilations that proved stale or wrong — with the evidence
1. **aviationmuseum.eu's Military Aviation Museum table is badly corrupted** and I refuse to use it. FAA cross-checks: it lists N108ZZ as a Lockheed PV-2 Harpoon — FAA says **Nord 1002**. N22518 as a Piper J-3 Super Cub — FAA says **North American SNJ-4**. N1639P as a Sopwith 1½ Strutter — FAA says **Polikarpov I-16 Type 24**. N183FW as a Flug Werk FW-190 — FAA says **Focke Wulf FW44J**. N23827 as a "Douglas A-4D Skyraider" — FAA says **Douglas AD-4**. Four to five verifiable errors out of five checks. Its serial column also mixes construction numbers and painted markings (P-51D "49-3087" is not a P-51D serial; I-15bis "49-1634" is nonsense).
2. **silverhawkauthor failed at Langley.** It places an F-4N BuNo 151510 and F-105D 61-0217 there; the official JBLE article gives **F-4C 64-0748** and Aerial Visuals gives **F-105D 61-0188**. It also credits Shannon Air Museum with "50+ aircraft including an F-14D and an A-4C" when the museum's own exhibits page lists five civil antiques. It is used here only for lead generation and, for Fort Eustis, for serials that are flagged as single-source on every row.
3. **Wikipedia's Aviation Historical Park list is stale.** It still lists a Douglas XAD-1 Skyraider that **left for the Intrepid Sea Air and Space Museum on 14 May 2014**, and it omits the A-7E that the newer list carries.
4. **militaryaircrafthistorian's Kestrel serial 64-18268 is impossible** — the XV-6A block is 64-18262 to 64-18267. Corrected to 64-18266 (XS692, NASA 520), agreed by Wikipedia, AirHistory.net and dated Flickr logs.
5. **HMdb's "permanently removed" flags at Air Power Park are about signage, not aircraft.** Nine single-aircraft markers show removed and eight paired markers show present — that is the 2024 refurbishment replacing old signs with new paired ones, not a collection dispersal. The park relaunched in May 2024 with *restored* aircraft.

### Production-block checks, as required

Passes are stated in each row's description. **Three failures are flagged in-row rather than silently dropped**: Oceana's F4D-1 141414 and F2H-4 127369 could not be matched to any published block for those variants; and Oceana's "F-4A 152295" cannot be an F-4A because 152295 sits inside the F-4B block 152207/152331 — I left `variant` blank there.
