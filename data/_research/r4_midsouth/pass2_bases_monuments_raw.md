### Sources used and the weight given to each
1. **OpenStreetMap Overpass** (`overpass.private.coffee`, plain tag queries, four state bboxes, September 2026 extract) — the currency spine. Plain `historic=aircraft` / `memorial=aircraft` / `memorial:type=aircraft` queries worked; the whole-region single query and one Tennessee query both died with `Dispatcher_Client::request_read_and_idx::timeout`, so the region had to be split into four (and Tennessee into two) sub-boxes with retries. OSM independently confirmed the presence of aircraft, in the current extract, at: Keesler air park (5 nodes with matching serials), Columbus AFB air park (2 nodes, matching serials), the Columbus highway T-37, Tupelo Veterans Park, George R. Hall Airpark Hattiesburg (3 nodes), VFW 6586 Sumrall (**`check_date=2024-10-16`** — the single best-dated currency in this pass), East Mississippi Electric Power Association, Sequatchie County Veterans Memorial Park (3 nodes), Patriot Park Covington, Mark Anton Airport Dayton, Veterans Museum Halls (2 nodes), Capt Jeff Kuss Memorial Smyrna, Graceland (2 nodes), Fort Campbell's Pratt air park (11 nodes) and Campbell AAF (2 nodes), the KY ARNG armory at Independence, Flying Tiger Heritage Park (all 5), NAS JRB New Orleans gate Hornet, Barksdale east-gate A-10, and American Legion Post 38 Baton Rouge.
2. **Aerial Visuals Locator + LocationDossier** — POSTed `Country=United States&Region=<state>&Scope=3` to the site-root `Locator.php`, then pulled 105 `LocationDossier.php?Serial=<id>` pages. This is the identity backbone: it is the only source giving per-airframe serials, c/ns and arc-second fixes for the monument sites. Vintage roughly 2008-2015 — treated as identity only, never currency.
3. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" (April 2016)** — retrieved via `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/.../aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf` and `pdftotext -layout`. Authoritative on USAF custody; it independently confirmed 44 of the airframes here and is the *later* of the two paper sources, so where it and Aerial Visuals disagree on presence it wins.
4. **Wikipedia** survivor lists — used only for the two AH-56A Cheyennes (66-8827 Fort Johnson, 66-8831 Fort Campbell), which it still lists as displayed.
5. **Fort Campbell / US Army and local press (2026)** — for the Pratt Museum closure and the new museum.
6. **Nominatim reverse geocoding** — to place three unlabelled OSM nodes.
7. **aviationmuseum.eu and airplanes-online** — consulted for England Airpark and immediately demonstrated the brief's warning. They give the Flying Tiger Heritage Park A-10 as **73-0667** and the F-105G as **56-3296**; both Aerial Visuals *and* the NMUSAF loan list give **73-1667** and **63-8296**. Digits are transposed in the lead lists. Their serials were discarded wholesale.

### Corrections made, with evidence
- **Flying Tiger Heritage Park Sabre.** Airplanes-online and aviationmuseum.eu call it "F-86 Sabre 52-4931". It is a **Canadair Sabre 5, ex-RCAF 23226, c/n 1016, ex-N46883**, painted to represent Lt Col John England's aircraft. The NMUSAF loan list books "F-86E, RCAF 23226" to the England district and the current OSM node reads `s/n 23226`. 52-4931 is recorded in `aliases` as the painted marking, per the painted-markings rule.
- **Flying Tiger A-7.** The lead lists say "A-7D 66-3234". NMUSAF and Aerial Visuals both say **A-7A BuNo 152660**. Recorded as the A-7A.
- **T-33A 53-6009.** NMUSAF (2016) books it to the *City of Johnson City*; Aerial Visuals shows Johnson City with the airframe struck off and the same serial live at *Bowser Ridge Model Airport, Kingsport*. One aircraft, two claims — recorded at Kingsport, flagged.
- **UH-1H 73-21688.** Aerial Visuals shows it struck off Gulfport CRTC (Mississippi) and live at the AASF at McKellar-Sipes (Tennessee). Recorded in Tennessee only.
- **F-15A 77-0067.** NMUSAF books it to LA ANG New Orleans 159 MXS but Aerial Visuals puts the airframe physically at Esler AAF / Camp Beauregard. Per the brief's "record where an aircraft IS, not who owns it", it is filed at Esler.
- **NAS Meridian T-2 159727.** Aerial Visuals labels it T-2A; 159727 is squarely in the T-2C production run. Variant corrected to C, serial untouched, discrepancy flagged in the row.

### The single most important currency finding
**The Don F. Pratt Memorial Museum closed permanently on 22 November 2024**, and the collection moved to the **Tennessee Wings of Liberty Museum**, which opened to the public on **15 May 2026** at 400 Wings of Liberty Way, Clarksville TN 37042 — a free, off-post, 33-acre site at Tiny Town Road and Fort Campbell Boulevard, open Tue-Sat 10:00-16:00, +1-270-798-8965. The Fort Campbell Historical Foundation states the new campus "**will eventually include** historic aircraft and other large artifacts", i.e. the outdoor aircraft are a *future* development there. The eleven OSM `historic=aircraft` nodes at the old on-post air park are still in the current extract and Wikipedia still lists AH-56A 66-8831 as displayed at Fort Campbell, so the air park is recorded as still standing on post behind the gate (`restricted`). **This is the number-one thing to verify by phone.** Only one aircraft is recorded at the new museum (the indoor MH-47) because no source names the rest.

### What was NOT reached
- **HMdb** was not paged through town by town. The budget went to Overpass, 105 Aerial Visuals dossiers and the NMUSAF PDF, which between them covered far more airframes per request. HMdb remains the best next source for dated catalogue stamps and inscription serials on the VFW / American Legion / county-park displays, and for finding monument sites that carry no aircraft-specific OSM tag.
- **Joe Baugher** per-serial pages were not opened; block checks above were done against published production blocks, not against individual disposition entries. Baugher would settle several of the flagged serials.
- **DVIDS** captions were not mined for dated base currency. This is the right tool for Keesler, Columbus AFB, Barksdale, NAS JRB New Orleans and Jackson-Evers, where base public-affairs photography is regularly published and dated.
