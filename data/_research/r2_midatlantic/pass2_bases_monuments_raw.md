## NOTES

### Sources and the weight given to each
1. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" (April 2016)** — pulled as a real PDF from
   `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/http://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Portals/7/documents/other/aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf`
   (note the path is `/other/`, not `/education/` — the `/education/` path returns a Wayback HTML shell).
   62 rows for NJ/MD/WV/DE/DC. Authoritative on custody and on USAF serials; useless on currency.
   This register is what settled Cumberland, Warfield, Milton, Vienna, Weirton, Pocomoke, Ellicott City,
   Burlington Township, Hamilton Township, Dover Post 2, New Castle, the 177th FW row, the DC ANG row
   at Andrews, Bolling, and the WV ANG P-51.
2. **Aerial Visuals Locator/LocationDossier** — POSTed to the site root with
   `Country=United States&Region=<state>&Scope=3`, then 59 per-location dossiers. It is the only source
   with arc-second fixes for most monument sites, and every coordinate above that is not credited to
   OSM or HMdb comes from it. Vintage roughly 2008-2015 — identity only, never currency.
3. **OpenStreetMap via overpass.private.coffee** — `historic=aircraft` and `memorial=aircraft` over
   bbox 37.8,-81.8,41.4,-74.8. The provider is heavily loaded; plain tag queries succeed only after
   retries and `aircraft:type` existence queries timed out repeatedly. 114 `historic=aircraft` and 5
   `memorial=aircraft` elements returned. This is the currency spine and it independently confirmed:
   Bolling F-105D, Andrews F6F and C-140 and UH-1N, Martinsburg F-86, Bethany Beach UH-1,
   Dover Post 2 T-33, Annapolis stadium Hornet, four Naval Academy airframes (including an EA-6B that
   no directory carries), the College Park UH-1, and the two unidentified DC Armory airframes.
4. **Wikipedia survivor/display lists** — F-86, F-4, C-141, KC-135, HU-16, F-100, F-14, UH-1.
   Added the Holmdel UH-1D serial, the Lakehurst air-park name, Sea Girt for F-4D 66-0259,
   and Starlifter Memorial Park for 66-7947.
5. **HMdb** — used for Guardian Park (marker 208227, catalogue stamp February 2023). The
   `results.asp?SearchFor=...&State=...` form does not filter on the keyword and returns the whole
   state list, so per-marker fetches were used instead.
6. **silverhawkauthor** and **Clio** — treated as lead lists. silverhawk supplied the Warfield 53-1411
   reading and the Fort Meade airframe list; Clio supplied the Milton park's name and street address.

  EA-3B 146448 and RU-8D as "airframes not on-site". NMUSAF still shows C-130A 57-0453 on loan to

### Deliberately blank fields
- `year_built` is blank on every row. No construction, delivery or acceptance date was sourced for any
  of these airframes, and a fiscal-year prefix is not a build year.
- Coordinates are blank for the New Jersey Vietnam Veterans Memorial (Holmdel) and the NY-56 launch
  area — no airframe-level or marker-level fix was obtained for either, and a wrong fix is worse.
- Tail numbers are blank for the Fort Meade Huey, the Weide TH-55, the College Park UH-1, the Andrews
  UH-1N, the Townsend Huey, the MXY-7 and both DC Armory airframes. No source publishes them.
- Variant is blank where the source gives only the base designation (McGuire UH-1 71-20326, Townsend).
