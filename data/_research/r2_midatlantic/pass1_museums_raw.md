### Deliberately blank fields

- **Almost every `tail_number` in the NASM Mall block.** The Smithsonian publishes an *inventory*
  number (A19610048000 style), not a military serial or civil registration, and I did not have the
  budget to open ~60 individual object pages. Well-known identities (Spirit of St. Louis N-X-211,
  X-1 46-062, X-15 56-6670, Bf 109 G-6 W.Nr. 160756) are **omitted from memory rather than written
  in**, per the never-invent rule. The inventory number is carried in `description` for every row so
  the next pass can resolve each one from airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects.
- **Goddard Visitor Center coordinates** — no fix on the rocket garden was obtained; a geocode of
  the visitor centre entrance would be a guess about where the rockets stand.
- **`year_built` is blank almost everywhere.** Fiscal-year prefixes were not converted. Where a year
  appears it came from an FAA `YEAR MFR` field or the Smithsonian's own object date.
- **Aviation Hall of Fame Convair 880, Cessna 150 and McCulloch J-2 at Wildwood, several Glenn L.
  Martin airframes** — listed by type with no identity anywhere.
