## NOTES

#### Sources and the weight given to each
- **Museum's own current pages (highest weight on presence).** neam.org/pages/aircraft, americanheritagemuseum.org/aircrafts, ctairandspace.org/aircraft, battleshipcove.org/aircraft, westfieldaviationmuseum.org/aircraft, bnamuseum.org, colemuseum.org/memorials, maineairmuseum.com, aviationmuseumofnh.org, vt.public.ng.mil/Museum. All fetched 2026-09-10 and all live.
- **FAA bulk registry** `ReleasableAircraft.zip`, MASTER.txt dated 2026-09-09, downloaded with a browser User-Agent (bare curl 403s as the brief warns). Filtered to CT/RI/MA/NH/VT/ME and scanned for museum-shaped owner names: 371 hits, of which ~60 are genuine museum holdings. This is the single strongest *currency* source in the pass — a registration still on a museum's name in the September 2026 file is near-proof the airframe has not left. It found the Dakota Aviation Museum, Maine Center for Classic Aviation, Tunison Foundation and Heritage Flight Foundation, none of which appear in the museum directories.
- **Aerial Visuals Locator** — POSTed to `Locator.php` at the site root with `Country=United States&Region=<State>&Scope=3`, then `LocationDossier.php?Serial=<id>`. All six states returned. Excellent for per-airframe serials, construction numbers and arc-second fixes; vintage ~2008-2015, so used as an identity source only. `LocationDossier.php?Serial=2071` (Connecticut Air and Space Center) throws a PHP fatal error server-side and could not be read.
- **OpenStreetMap** via `overpass.private.coffee`. The bare-tag query works; a six-state bounding box times out, so it was split into five smaller boxes and even then Maine needed a node-only query. Only ~20 `historic=aircraft` objects exist across all six states — New England is very thinly mapped — but the ones that exist are current (2026-07 extract) and confirm the NEAM outdoor A-3B, C-7A and HU-16E and the Nashua A-4.
- **Wikipedia** collection lists for NEAM, Owls Head, CASC, AHM, Maine Air Museum, Brunswick and Quonset.
- **silverhawkauthor.com** used strictly as a lead list per the brief. Its Rhode Island page proved genuinely useful on Quonset dispersal destinations; its Maine and Massachusetts pages contain obvious errors (see corrections).

#### Corrections made with evidence
1. **NEAM UH-1B**: Wikipedia gives 62-02550. No UH-1B block contains that number. Aerial Visuals gives 62-12550, which sits inside the valid UH-1B block 62-12515/62-12556. Recorded as 62-12550, Wikipedia's reading rejected.
2. **NEAM F-104**: Aerial Visuals types it F-104D-5-LO; 56-0901 sits inside the F-104C block 56-0883/56-0938 and no F-104D carries an FY56 serial. Recorded as F-104C.
3. **Brunswick Neptune**: SilverHawk gives BuNo 131427. That airframe was scrapped at Quonset in May 2018 (Wikipedia, Quonset Air Museum). Aerial Visuals gives 128392 for Brunswick, which block-checks as a P2V-5/SP-2E. Recorded as 128392 and flagged.
4. **Bennington Museum "Martin Wasp"**: the surviving Bennington-built Wasp is a **motor car**, not an aeroplane. The Martin Wasp Corporation made both, and directories conflate them. Not recorded — a trap avoided.
5. **NEAM B-25H 43-4999 vs Collings "Tondelayo"**: N3970C / c/n 98-22000 appears in the *NEAM* Aerial Visuals dossier, not the Collings one. The Collings B-25 is therefore recorded with a blank serial rather than being given NEAM's identity.
6. **Pioneer Valley Military & Transportation Museum = Westfield Aviation Museum.** The FAA registers four airframes to the former name and one to the latter, at the same field. Aerial Visuals carries them as two locations (2816 and 13936). Treated as one site under the public-facing name.

#### Judgment calls
- **Owls Head count.** Wikipedia lists 22-24 aircraft; the FAA registry shows 17 airframes currently registered to the museum; Aerial Visuals claims "70+". I counted **31**: the 17 FAA-current registrations, plus the Aerial Visuals main-building entries not in the FAA file (Deperdussin, Farman III, Wright Flyer replica, Bleriot XI), plus the Wikipedia types with no registration found (Tiger Moth, T-6G, J-3 Cub, M-18 Mite, Great Lakes 2T-1A, Bushmaster 2000, Clark ornithopter, Domenjoz glider, Milliken Special, Keane Ace). Many are maintained airworthy and flown at summer events — they are still museum collection pieces, not private warbirds merely based at a field, so they are recorded.
- **Collings Foundation kept as a site separate from the American Heritage Museum.** They share one campus (Aerial Visuals puts both at 42.4022/-71.5079) but have different mailing towns, different access, and different aircraft: AHM's hall is walk-up ticketed, the Collings flying collection is not routinely on public display. Aircraft that AHM's own 2026 page lists are filed under AHM; the touring/airworthy fleet is filed under Collings with `appointment` access. A reconciler that prefers one site can merge them; a reconciler that prefers to drop airworthy touring aircraft can drop the Collings block cleanly.
- **Winvian Farm** recorded. It is a resort, not a museum, but a complete HH-3F Pelican built into a guest cottage is exactly the "restaurants, rooftops" class the brief asks for. Access is `appointment` because only guests reach it.
- **Camp Johnson access**. The Vermont Military Museum posts public hours and sits on an open Guard post with no manned gate; recorded `public`, not `restricted`. Worth a confirming call.

#### EXCLUDED — do not re-research
- **Quonset Air Museum, North Kingstown RI — CLOSED and dispersed.** Roof partially collapsed under snow in March 2015, building condemned; a $4m state request failed in June 2016; officials announced on 16 December 2016 it would not reopen; the ~28 aircraft were dispersed 2017-18. Traced destinations: **XF15C-1 BuNo 01215 → Hickory Aviation Museum NC**; **A-6B 155629 → Hickory NC**; **F6F-5 70185 → National Naval Aviation Museum FL**; **A-4F 155027 (Blue Angels) → Southern Museum of Flight AL**; **TBM-3E 53914 → CAF Arizona Wing**; **AD-5W/A-1E 135188 → Collings Foundation, Stow MA** (recorded above); **Stinson 10A → Maine Air Museum, Bangor** (recorded above, moved 1999); **P2V/SP-2E 131427 scrapped on site May 2018**. F3D-2Q 124620 and F-14B 162591 destinations not established. No successor aviation museum exists in Rhode Island.
- **New England Escadrille, Fitchburg MA** — closed; its C-47 was sold.
- **World's Smallest Aviation Museum, Leominster MA** — closed.
- **Yesterday's Wings Aero Museum, Hampton NH** — closed.
- **American Museum of Aviation / Prop-Liners of America, Hartford Brainard CT** — never formed in Connecticut; the project relocated to Las Vegas NV.
