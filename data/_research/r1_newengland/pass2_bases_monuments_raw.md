## NOTES

#### Sources and the weight given to each
- **OpenStreetMap Overpass** (overpass.private.coffee, plain tag queries, bbox 40.9,-73.8 to 47.5,-66.8, run September 2026). 22 elements for historic=aircraft / memorial=aircraft / memorial:type=aircraft; a second sweep for historic=missile, memorial=missile, memorial=plane, historic=helicopter and tourism=attraction+aircraft:type returned no aircraft at all (only eight tank monuments). This was treated as the ONLY genuinely current source and is what carries the currency claims for Brunswick, Pease, Nashua, Middlebury, Waterbury, Middletown, Beverly, Shea Field, Barnes, Bradley (C-130) and the single Burlington F-16.
- **Aerial Visuals** Locator (POST to the site root with Country/Region/Scope=3) plus per-location LocationDossier. Used for identities, c/ns and arc-second fixes only. Vintage roughly 2008-2015; several of its entries are demonstrably stale (see corrections).
- **Joe Baugher** USAF and USN serial pages. The live site 404s on 1949, 1951, 1952, 1953, 1956, 1957, 1966, 1967, 1968 and 1976; the crouze.com mirror is dead (all paths 404). Those years were pulled from `web.archive.org/web/2023id_/` **with `curl --compressed`** - without it the body comes back as raw gzip. Even so the archived 1956 page covers only 56-0001/0956 and the archived 1966 page stops in the C-141 block, so 56-1264, 56-3008, 66-0240, 66-14390, 66-15325, 66-16132 and 66-16836 could not be disposition-checked. They were block-checked by hand instead and the gap is stated in each row.
- **FAA bulk registry** (ReleasableAircraft.zip, already unpacked in work/faa). Confirmed the brief's warning: it found no monument sites at all. It did produce two useful negatives (below).
- **Press Herald** 26 July 2025 for Brunswick; **vt.public.ng.mil** museum Displays page for the Camp Johnson F-4; **Wikipedia** for the Loring Hound Dog; **loringairmuseum.com** and **gmvmm.com** for their own sites.
- **silverhawkauthor** used as a LEAD LIST ONLY and every serial it gave was dropped or re-anchored. Its Massachusetts page gives the Barnes F-84F as 51-9480 and the Hanscom F-86H as 53-1328 - both are painted numbers, not identities, exactly the failure mode the brief warns about.
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location"**: NOT REACHED. The archived path in the brief now returns the Wayback wrapper page rather than a PDF, and a CDX sweep of 200 archived nationalmuseum.af.mil PDFs plus probes of the LinkClick fileticket URLs turned up the AFI 84-103 instruction and the loan-programme FAQ but not the by-location table. Custody attributions for the Otis, Barnes, Bradley and Burlington airframes are therefore unverified.
- **HMdb**: three town/keyword queries (Waterbury CT, Connecticut keyword search) returned no aviation markers at all. HMdb does not appear to catalogue these New England airframe monuments, so the per-town sweep was abandoned as low-yield.

#### Deliberately blank fields
- `year_built` is blank on every row. Not one source consulted gave a construction, delivery or acceptance date, and the brief forbids deriving it from a fiscal-year prefix.
- `tail_number` is blank on the Groton Huey (Aerial Visuals' "512776 USN" is not a valid Huey BuNo), the Otis Huey (three candidate identities), the Otis F-86 (painted serial only), both Camp Johnson T-33s, the Middletown Huey, the Hanscom P-40 replica and the Loring Hound Dog.
- `wing_type` is blank on every rotary_wing and on the missile, per the brief.
- Street addresses and postal codes are blank for the base air parks and most post displays - no source gave one and a geocoded guess would be worse than nothing.
- No Nike missile record exists anywhere in this pass. New England was ringed with Nike sites but the OSM missile sweep returned nothing, and no search surfaced a *displayed* missile in the six states. Bare plaques at former launch sites do not qualify.
