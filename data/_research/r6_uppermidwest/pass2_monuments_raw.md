## NOTES

### Sources and the weight given to each

1. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location", July 2015 edition** — pulled through
   `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/.../aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf` and run through
   pdftotext. The single most productive source for this pass, exactly as the task predicted: 174
   rows across the four states, and the civic-loan rows (CITY OF …, AMERICAN LEGION POST …,
   VFW POST …, county authorities) map almost one-to-one onto the monument sites here. It is
   **authoritative on custody and serial and worthless on currency** — it is eleven years old.
2. **Aerial Visuals** — `Locator.php` POSTed at the site root with `Country=United States`,
   `Region=<state>`, `Scope=3`, then `LocationDossier.php?Serial=<id>` for each of 104 locations.
   This supplied the arc-second fixes for essentially every coordinate in the SITES block, plus
   construction numbers, civil registrations and the "moved on / not on-site" flags that let several
   phantom sites be struck. Vintage roughly 2008-2015: an identity and geometry source, never a
   currency source.
3. **OpenStreetMap Overpass** via `overpass.private.coffee` — `historic=aircraft`,
   `memorial=aircraft`, `memorial:type=aircraft`, queried per state. The dispatcher was heavily
   loaded and returned `Dispatcher_Client::request_read_and_idx::timeout` repeatedly; the four
   states needed retry loops and South Dakota had to be split into two half-width bboxes before it
   would answer. This is the **only current** source used and it independently confirms the
   presence in 2026 of: Thief River Falls A-4C, Willmar F-14A, Sheldon T-33A, Correctionville
   A-7D and F-84F, Fessenden F-100D, Oakes F-100F, Valley City F-104C, Lisbon UH-1H,
   Chamberlain T-33A, Yankton T-38A. Those ten are the strongest records in the file.
4. **National Park Service** pages for Minuteman Missile NHS, read September 2026, for Delta-09.
5. HMdb was tried for Marshalltown and returned only a Freedom Rock marker — the upper-Midwest
   aircraft monuments are largely uncatalogued there, so it earned nothing this pass.

### Deliberately blank fields

- `year_built` is blank throughout. No sourced construction, delivery or acceptance date was found
  for any airframe in this pass, and a fiscal-year serial prefix is not a build year.
- `postal_code`, `address` and `website` are blank for every monument site except Minuteman Missile
  NHS. None of the sources used publishes a street address for a plinth in a town park, and a
  geocoded guess would be worse than a blank when an arc-second airframe fix is already present.
- Serials are blank for the Brainerd F9F Panther, the Cedar Rapids rooftop PA-22, the Cavalier AFS
  Spartan, the Delta-09 Minuteman II and the Emmons Huey. In every case a serial was either absent
  from all sources or - at Emmons - present but impossible.
- Construction numbers written as `c/n unknown` in `aliases` where the source gave a serial but no
  c/n; these should be emptied by the reconciler if it prefers a true blank.

news, Wikipedia survivor lists or Joe Baugher's disposition pages was possible; WebFetch on known
URLs was the only live-web tool available. In practice that removed the ", MN" / ", IA" / ", ND" /
