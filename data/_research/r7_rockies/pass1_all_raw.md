# NOTES

## Sources used and how much weight each carried
- **OpenStreetMap via overpass.private.coffee** — the only genuinely current positional source in this pass. Montana returned 16 nodes/ways; **Wyoming returned zero** (verified with a clean re-run after an initial Overpass XML error page); Alaska returned 24 across four longitude slices. Alaska node IDs in the 12457xxxxxx and 13950xxxxxx ranges are 2024-2025 surveys and several carry the USAF serial in `description`, which made them a rare case of OSM supplying identity as well as currency. The Aleutian slice west of the dateline (51.0,170.0,72.0,180.0) **timed out and was never retrieved**.
- **Aerial Visuals Locator** (root `Locator.php`, POST, `Scope=3`) — 32 Montana locations, 18 Wyoming, 85 Alaska. Best per-airframe identity and arc-second fixes. Vintage ~2008-2015; used for identity and coordinates only, never for currency.
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location"** (July 2015 stamp, via `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/`) — 32 Alaska rows, 20 Montana rows, 7 Wyoming rows. Authoritative on custody and it corroborated essentially every military serial Aerial Visuals gave. Its serials are typographically lossy (`59-069`, `47-637`, `65-956`, `53-360`); each was expanded to the only serial that fits the production block and the expansion is stated in the row.
- **FAA bulk registry** (`ReleasableAircraft.zip` with a browser User-Agent; MASTER joined to ACFTREF) — 181 museum-shaped owner rows across the three states. Excellent for Alaska Aviation Museum and Museum of Mountain Flying; found nothing for monument sites, as expected. Filtering hard was necessary in Alaska: the University of Alaska Fairbanks alone contributed ~40 drone registrations.
- **Dated web sources** — *The Electric* (Great Falls) 11 August 2026 on the Lions Park F-102 repaint; Tripadvisor review dated September 2026 for Malmstrom Museum; AOPA feature September 2025 for Alaska Aviation Museum; warrenmuseum.com read September 2026 for base-access rules; malmstrom.af.mil for hours.
- **Wikipedia survivor lists** — used only to corroborate serials already held (A-7, F-101, T-33). They are thin for these three states.
- aviationmuseum.eu and silverhawkauthor appeared in results and were **not used**.

## Corrections made, with evidence
- **F-86L 53-0806** is claimed by three Aerial Visuals locations (Cheyenne Regional; Wyoming ANG; Wyoming NG HQ Guernsey). Cheyenne Regional and Wyoming ANG both mark it "moved on"; only Guernsey carries a current arc-second fix. Recorded at **Camp Guernsey** and flagged.
- **F-102A 56-1116** and **T-33B 141549** are both listed at the University of Montana Helena College as "moved on". Re-homed to the 120th AW air park and Miracle of America Museum respectively, following the receiving sites' own fixes.
- **T-33A 56-3661** was "once stored" at the Wyoming ANG compound; recorded at the **Wyoming National Guard Museum** on Bishop Boulevard, which is where the current fix is.
- **C-123J 56-4395** was recorded by Aerial Visuals at the Kulis ANG museum; a dated contributor note says it moved on 17 April 2018 to 37th Street and Airlifter Drive on Elmendorf. Recorded at the JBER 144th AS air park.
- OSM mislabels three Great Falls ANG airframes (F-16C for F-16A 79-0290, F-15C for F-15A 74-0112, F-106 for F-102A 56-1116). Aerial Visuals and the NMUSAF loan list agree against OSM, so the OSM type tags were discarded and only their positions kept.
- The **Malmstrom UH-1F** NMUSAF row reads `65-956`. The UH-1F block is 65-7911 to 65-7960; `65-7956` (Aerial Visuals, with c/n 7097) is the only reading that fits.

## Serial block checks
All serials above were checked against their production blocks and pass, with four exceptions flagged in their rows:
- **T-33B BuNo 141549** (Miracle of America) sits at or past the edge of the published T-33B BuNo run; the c/n 580-9605 is consistent with a T-33 but the BuNo needs an independent check. Aerial Visuals separately lists a "T-33 54-1549" at the same museum, which looks like the same aircraft entered twice with the digits re-cut; only one T-33 is recorded here.
- **P-40E 40-0598** (Alaska Aviation Museum) does not sit cleanly in the P-40E block. Kept as given because it is a recovered-wreck identity, and flagged.
- **H-21B 52-8696 vs 53-4369** on JBER Richardson — NMUSAF and Aerial Visuals say 52-8696, a 2025 OSM node at the same spot says 53-4369. Both are valid H-21B serials. Recorded as 52-8696 on custody evidence and flagged.
- **F-86E RCAF 23351 (1141)** at the Wyoming ANG is a Canadair Sabre with no USAF serial; `tail_number` carries the RCAF number.

## Deliberately blank fields
- `year_built` is blank on every military airframe. No sourced construction, delivery or acceptance date was found for any of them, and the FAA `YEAR MFR` field was only available for the civil-registered museum aircraft, where it was used.
- Missile rows carry no `tail_number`: neither F.E. Warren nor Malmstrom publishes serials or lot numbers for its displayed ICBMs.
- The Fairbanks terminal Jenny carries no serial — original-versus-reproduction is unresolved.
- The Boniface Gate F-15 carries no `tail_number`: it is a mockup with no airframe serial; `43-0139` is the painted marking and sits in `aliases`.
- `postal_code` on base sites is the base's ZIP, not a verified per-display code.

## Ranked open questions
1. **The Museum of Flight and Aerial Firefighting, Greybull, Wyoming** (South Bighorn County Airport, 44.5106,-108.0862). Aerial Visuals lists 100+ airframes including six KC-97Ls, eight C-130As, eight P-2 Neptunes, five C-119s and two P4Y-2 Privateers — potentially the single largest site in this whole pass.
