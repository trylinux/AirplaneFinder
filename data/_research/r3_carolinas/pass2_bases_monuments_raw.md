## NOTES

### Sources and the weight given to each
1. **OpenStreetMap via overpass.private.coffee** — plain-tag POST queries for `historic=aircraft` and
   `memorial=aircraft` over bbox 32.0,-84.4,36.6,-75.4 (the `memorial:type` query returned an
   Overpass dispatcher timeout twice and was abandoned; `memorial=aircraft` covers the same ground).
   59 elements after clipping Georgia. This is the **currency spine**: several NC/SC clusters were
   surveyed and serial-tagged in 2024-2025 (Seymour Johnson, Shaw, Joint Base Charleston, Fort Bragg
   x2, New River, Cherry Point, Havelock, Bessemer City, Enka, Greensboro, Gaffney, Camden, Easley,
   Greenville). Where an OSM node dated 2024-2025 carries a serial that matches Aerial Visuals and
   NMUSAF, I have treated the airframe as confirmed present.
2. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location"** (April 2016), fetched via
   `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/…`, pdftotext -layout. 28 NC rows and 28 SC rows.
   **Authoritative on custody and on USAF serials**; it corrected/confirmed serials at Seymour
   Johnson, Shaw, McEntire, Joint Base Charleston, Pope, Bessemer City, Gaffney, Camden, Darlington,
   Hartsville, Goldsboro, Greenville, Myrtle Beach, the Citadel, NC A&T. It supplied the **VFW post
   number at Wilmington (Post 2573)** which Aerial Visuals lacks.
3. **Aerial Visuals** (site-root `Locator.php` POST, then 79 `LocationDossier.php` fetches).
   The identity and coordinate backbone — arc-second fixes, c/ns, BuNos. Vintage ~2008-2015; used
   for identity and position, never on its own as evidence of presence.
4. **FAA bulk registry** (`ReleasableAircraft.zip` with a browser User-Agent; MASTER.txt joined to
   ACFTREF.txt). Used to test civil registrations. It produced three real removals (below).
5. **HMdb** — markers at Goldsboro (F-86H, catalogued Feb 2025), the Citadel AH-1 (revised Feb 2023),
   New River Aviation Memorial (revised Nov 2024), Pope AFB Air Park (2011/2016).
6. Dated feature articles and official pages: the 82nd Airborne Division Museum's own airpark page,
   the Joint Base air park fact sheet, Stars and Stripes on the Beaufort F-35B (Sept 2025), a
   Suburban Living feature on the Fuquay-Varina C-54 (Sept 2025), an aviationhistorymuseums.com
   visit report on Havelock/Cherry Point (June 2022).

  survey of the memorial park, which recorded exactly five aircraft.

  record and reported gone in another, and absent from OSM.
