Authoritative for N-number, c/n and `YEAR-MFR`. Produced the Western NC Air Museum's 14 registrations, the three Asheboro registrations, the Sullenberger DC-3 (N44V c/n 4545, still registered to "Carolinas Aviation Museum"), the Sabre Society of North Carolina (Hickory's parent) Aeronca N31873, and **the whole Celebrate Freedom Foundation fleet**, which no directory had surfaced.

4. Wikipedia articles (Sullenberger, Hickory, NC Aviation Museum, NCTM); dated 2025 news (Axios Charlotte, General Aviation News, Vintage Aviation News, WUNC).

**Corrections made, with evidence**
