### Sources and their weight
1. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location"**, April 2016 edition, retrieved as a real PDF from
   `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/http://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Portals/7/documents/other/aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf`
   (note the `/Portals/7/` path — the bare `/documents/other/` path in the task brief 404s to a
   Wayback shell). 69 Illinois rows, 47 Indiana rows, 46 Michigan rows. Authoritative on USAF
   custody and serial, useless on currency. This is the backbone of the monument set.
2. **Aerial Visuals Locator**, POST to the site root with `Country=United States&Region=<state>&Scope=3`,
   then `LocationDossier.php?Serial=<id>`. 153 Illinois / 95 Indiana / 102 Michigan locations;
   ~120 dossiers pulled. Gives arc-second fixes on individual airframes plus c/n and civil marks.
   Vintage roughly 2008-2015 — identity source only.
3. **OpenStreetMap** via `overpass.private.coffee`. Illinois returned 51 elements, Indiana 46.
   **Michigan only partially returned**: the dispatcher repeatedly answered
   `Dispatcher_Client::request_read_and_idx::timeout` on any Michigan-wide box, across ~8 attempts
   with 25-45 s backoff. One sub-box (41.6/-84.5 to 43.5/-82.3) succeeded and returned 32 nodes,
   all of them the Selfridge Military Air Museum air park plus the Frankenmuth Sabre. Two further
   sub-boxes came back late: 43.5/-85.5 to 45.5/-82.3 (2 nodes: the Gaylord Westwind monument and
   the Grayling American Legion T-33) and 41.6/-84.5 to 43.5/-87.0 (12 nodes: the Air Zoo, plus
   Hidden Cove Park and VFW Post 6248 at Decatur). Four sub-boxes never returned — the western
   Lower Peninsula above Kalamazoo, the whole Upper Peninsula, and the two south-west boxes.
   Alternative endpoints are unusable from this container: `overpass-api.de` resets the connection,
   `overpass.kumi.systems` times out, `overpass.osm.jp` has an expired certificate.
   **Consequence: most Michigan monuments in this file — and every Upper Peninsula record,
   including the whole K I Sawyer group — have NO 2023-2026 currency counterweight.**
4. `silverhawkauthor.com` used strictly as a lead list, per the brief. It produced three leads

### The question the task asked about Rantoul
The Chanute Air Museum closed permanently on **1 November 2015**. Dated visitor reports
(Bob Farquhar, 13 May 2016: crews dismantling the flight-line display aircraft; Chet Cybulski,
28 April 2021: "all the airplanes are no longer there") confirm the outdoor park is gone.
**No aircraft remain at Rantoul.** What physically remains is the **Minuteman I** on its stand at
the west entrance of the former base — slated for demolition on 14 September 2020 and reprieved
after a public campaign. It is recorded above; a coordinate was not obtainable and is left blank.
Two ex-Chanute airframes were positively re-located in this pass and are recorded at their new
homes: **GF-111A 63-9767** at the Lake County Veterans Memorial in Waukegan, and **F-86A 47-0615**
and **GT-33A 52-9797** at the Indiana ANG 122nd Fighter Wing at Fort Wayne. The other 22 Chanute
airframes on the 2016 NMUSAF register are unaccounted for by this pass and are the single largest
open question in Illinois.

### The question the task asked about Wurtsmith and K I Sawyer
**Wurtsmith / Oscoda: there is no preserved B-52.** The Wurtsmith Air Museum collection is small
aircraft, two T-33s, a Huey, an L-19 and a CG-4A. The Wurtsmith B-52D **55-0677** went to Belleville,
Michigan (Yankee / Michigan Flight Museum, another pass's site) — it is on the 2016 NMUSAF register
under Belleville with no organisation named.
**K I Sawyer: yes.** B-52D **55-0062** stands on the former base at 46.3419,-87.3831 with an
F-106A, F-101B, FB-111A, T-33A and an ADM-20 Quail. NMUSAF books the group to Air Force
Association Chapter 238 at Marquette.

### Production block checks
All serials above pass a block check against their type's known FY blocks. No serial was altered.
Three entries carry no serial at all rather than a guess: the Camp Atterbury Huey, the Oxford Cobra
and the second Camp Lincoln Huey. The Camp Atterbury Huey is the notable case — the serial widely
quoted for it, **66-10584**, is an AGM-12 Bullpup number and cannot be a Huey; Aerial Visuals'
location log carries Kevin Kuhn's analysis of this. `tail_number` left blank deliberately.

### Deliberately blank fields
- `year_built` is blank on **every** row. No sourced construction, delivery or acceptance date was
  found for any airframe here, and per the brief a fiscal-year serial prefix is never a build year.
- `postal_code` is blank where no street address was available.
- Coordinates are blank for American Legion Post 263 at Lincoln and for the Rantoul Minuteman.
  A blank beats a wrong fix.
- `address` is blank for most post displays; the arc-second fix is the better locator.
