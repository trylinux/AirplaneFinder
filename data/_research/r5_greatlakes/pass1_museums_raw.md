#### Access-type judgment calls
- **Selfridge Military Air Museum → `restricted`.** The task brief called it publicly accessible, but the museum sits inside Selfridge ANGB and visitors pass a controlled military gate. Under the brief's own definition ("behind a controlled/military gate") that is `restricted`. If the project's convention is to treat escorted-but-advertised base museums as `public`, flip this one field.
