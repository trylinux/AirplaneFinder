NOTES

## What this pass added
7 sites and 16 airframes: 6 sites / 15 airframes in Wyoming, 1 site / 1 airframe in
New Hampshire, and **nothing new that could be confirmed in Rhode Island or West
Virginia**. See the per-state sections below for why.

## Sources used and their weight
- **Aerial Visuals** (`Locator.php` / `LocationDossier.php` POSTed at the site root
  with `Country=United States&Region=<State>&Scope=3`) — worked for all four states
  and produced the complete site inventory this pass is built on. Note the site
  rate-limits: a burst of dossier fetches returns a 102-byte `429 Too Many Requests`
  body that looks like a successful fetch. 16 of 52 dossiers came back as 429 on the
  first pass and had to be refetched with a 1-2 s delay.
- **Aerial Visuals `AirframeDossier.php?Serial=<id>`** — the decisive tool this pass.
  Each airframe dossier carries a **"Latest Owner or Location"** field that is
  maintained well past the ~2008-2015 vintage of the location pages. This is the
  cheapest way to test whether an Aerial Visuals location record is stale, and it
  overturned two records here (see Corrections). Dossier ids are scraped out of the
  `LocationDossier.php` HTML.
- **FAA bulk registry** (`ReleasableAircraft.zip`, browser User-Agent, joined
  `MASTER.txt` to `ACFTREF.txt` locally) — downloaded and used. Confirmed the Dakota
  Aviation Museum C-47 as a live registration at a real street address, confirmed
  PB4Y-2 N7962C is still physically at Greybull, and disproved Wikipedia's KC-97
  registration for Greybull. As the brief predicted it found no monument sites.
- **OpenStreetMap via the private.coffee Overpass mirror** — the currency
  counterweight. Plain tag queries only, small bboxes. The mirror was heavily loaded
  and returned `Dispatcher_Client::request_read_and_idx::timeout` frequently; a
  3-attempt retry loop with a 15-20 s backoff got most tiles through.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding** — very effective and cheap. Feeding
  the Aerial Visuals arc-second fixes to `/reverse` returns both a street address for
  the SITES row *and* the name of the OSM feature at that exact point, which is how
  the Cheyenne gate-guard discrepancy was found.
- **Wikipedia** — museum collection lists and the state museum lists.
- **aviationmuseum.eu** — used strictly as a lead list per the brief. Its US index
  yielded nothing for these four states that Aerial Visuals had not already given.

## Corrections made with evidence
1. **F-84F 52-7019 is NOT at the Wyoming Air National Guard gate at Cheyenne.** The
   Aerial Visuals *location* page still shows it there, but its *airframe* dossier
   gives "Latest Owner or Location: Western Nebraska Technical College, Sidney, NE".
   Whatever is on that plinth now is an F-86-shaped airframe per the current OSM node,
   so the row is recorded with a **blank tail_number**. Not "fixed" by borrowing
   53-0806, which belongs to Camp Guernsey.
2. **Lockheed SP-2H Neptune 143173 (N173AM) has left Greybull.** Aerial Visuals'
   location page lists it as museum-retained; its airframe dossier gives "Latest Owner
   or Location: Richard C. Camp, Lewes, DE". Excluded. This is very likely the
   "Lockheed P2V-7 Neptune" that Wikipedia still lists in the Greybull collection, so
   that Wikipedia line should be treated as stale.
3. **Wikipedia's Greybull KC-97 registration N1365N is wrong.** The FAA registry puts
   N1365N (s/n 52-2698) with the Berlin Airlift Historical Foundation in Farmingdale
   New Jersey. Aerial Visuals' museum-retained KC-97G is 52-0918 / c/n 16612 / N97HB
   and that airframe's dossier confirms the museum as its latest location. 52-0918 is
   used; N1365N is not.

## Judgment calls
- **The Greybull stored fleet is deliberately NOT recorded.** Aerial Visuals lists
  ~25 further Hawkins and Powers airframes "stored nearby" at Greybull. Wikipedia's
  Greybull article, citing Cowboy State Daily, says sixteen of the former firefighting
  aircraft went to **auction in 2025 with $25 starting bids**, and the FAA registry now
  shows the survivors (N397HP, N497HP, N8094, N37636, N8505A, N97HB) registered to a
  private owner with a Riverton mailing address. That fleet is actively dispersing and
  is storage/inventory rather than public display. Only the nine airframes that both
  Aerial Visuals' museum-retained list and Wikipedia's collection list agree on are
  recorded, all as `on_display`. **This whole site should be re-checked in 6-12 months.**
- **C-130 variant at the Cheyenne ANG gate left as E with serial 63-7861.** Two
  independent sources agree an airframe is physically there (OSM node, current) and
  Aerial Visuals' airframe dossier still names the 153rd as its location and supplies
  the serial. Only the variant letter is disputed (OSM says "C130H"), which reads as a
  mapper generalising rather than a different airframe, so the sourced serial is kept
  and the disagreement recorded in the description.
- **`year_built` is blank on every row except the Dakota C-47**, where it comes from
  the FAA `YEAR MFR` field (1944). No fiscal-year prefix was promoted to a build year.
- **Addresses left blank** for VFW Post 9439 Casper (only the nearest street name,
  East Sunlight Drive, could be reverse-geocoded — not the post's own address) and a
  blank postal code for the Cheyenne ANG gate display (Nominatim returns "82099",
  which is not a usable delivery ZIP, and the 153rd's own ZIP could not be sourced).
  Coordinates on every row are Aerial Visuals arc-second fixes on the airframe itself.

## EXCLUDED — do not re-research these
**New Hampshire**
- **Lebanon UH-1V 66-16836** — the Aerial Visuals airframe dossier says in plain text
  "**Only the cabin survives here**". A cabin section is a hulk, not a display. No site
  name was ever attached to it; the fix (43.65299, -72.24336) is a stretch of Hanover
  Street 2.5 km from American Legion Post 22, which is the only veterans post in
  Lebanon and is at 189 Mechanic Street.
- **Manchester Airport Jet Provost T.5A XW437 (N80873)**, **Boire Field T-28A 49-1520
  (N7038U)** and **HS-125 XW788 (N4QB)**, **Laconia JRB-1 (N67929)**, **Jaffrey Yak-52
  (N3043R)** — airworthy civil-registered warbirds merely based at a field. Aerial
  Visuals itself flags each with "these airframes *may* be based here". N7038U is
  FAA-registered to an owner in Cottage Grove **Wisconsin** and N67929 to a private
  owner in Loudon NH, confirming these are not displays.
- **Mt Success DC-3 crash site (42-68818)** and the **Northeast Airlines Flight 792
  crash site** (an OSM `memorial=aircraft` way) — wreck sites, excluded by the brief.
- **Atlantic Warbirds**, **Yesterdays Wings Aero Museum**, **Hampton Airfield** — no
  airframes listed on their Aerial Visuals pages; the Hampton C-47 has moved to the
  Dakota Aviation Museum, which is recorded here.
- **Aviation Museum of New Hampshire / Slusser Aviation Learning Center** — already
  flagged erroneous upstream; OSM confirms the buildings but no full-size airframe.

**Rhode Island**
- **Quonset Air Museum** — closed. Roof collapsed under snow in March 2015, hangar
  condemned, and on 16 December 2016 it was announced it would not reopen. Its P2V
  (131427) was **scrapped on site in May 2018** as it was too large to move. The
  remaining ~15 airframes Aerial Visuals still shows "on site" (F-4B 148371, F2H-3
  126292, A-4C 147790, AV-8A 158710, T-38A 63-8197, OH-6A 67-16570, AH-1S 66-15317,
  AH-1F 67-15530, OH-58A 70-15117, UH-1C 66-15083, O-2A 68-10997, T-33 137936, Lim-5M
  325, and a duplicated A-37/T-37B pair both carrying 57-2291) are gone. Aerial Visuals
  itself appeals for disposition information on that block. **No destination could be
  established for any of them and none could be shown to have stayed in Rhode Island.**
- **Richmond Airport An-2T (N6216C)** and **Westerly State Airport** — a based civil
  aircraft and a location with no airframes listed.
- **USS John F Kennedy Aircraft Carrier Project** — its only airframe (F9F Panther
  123092) is listed by Aerial Visuals as not on site.

**West Virginia**
- **Charleston "P-51 at Yeager Airport"** — the only evidence is an **8 January 2008**
  visitor note on the Aerial Visuals page. Eighteen years stale, and the coordinate
  resolves to 1557 Dixie Street in Charleston's East End, not the airport. Almost
  certainly a privately owned airworthy Mustang.
- **North Central West Virginia Airport (Bridgeport)** and **Ohio County Airport
  (Wheeling)** — both list only airframes Aerial Visuals marks as departed.
- **"Quick Silver" F-51D 45-11430 (N51HY)** near Lewisburg — an airworthy private

- **McLaughlin ANGB / 130th Airlift Wing, Charleston** — swept for. The Aerial Visuals
  location page for the 130th lists **no airframes at all**, the Wikipedia article for
  the wing mentions no static display or gate guardian, and OSM has no aircraft node
  inside the base. There is no evidence the 130th has a display aircraft.

## What was not reached
- **WebSearch was exhausted at the 200-call session cap before this pass issued a
  single query**, so no general web searching was possible at any point. Every
  substitute was blocked as well: DuckDuckGo HTML and lite endpoints, Mojeek (403)
  and searx.be (browser-verification interstitial). All discovery therefore ran off
  Aerial Visuals, OSM/Overpass, Nominatim, the FAA registry and direct WebFetch of
  URLs that could be guessed or found by link-scraping. **The New Hampshire, Rhode
  Island and West Virginia gaps are the direct cost of that** — those three states
  need exactly the local-news and VFW/Legion-post searching that was unavailable.
- **HMdb was not usefully worked.** The parameter guess `results.asp?SearchFor=` is
  wrong — it returns all 961 New Hampshire markers unfiltered rather than a keyword
  match. The brief's `results.asp?Search=Place&Town=<town>&State=<State>` form needs a
  town per query, and with no search engine there was no way to generate the town list
  worth querying. A town-by-town HMdb sweep of New Hampshire and Rhode Island is the
  obvious next move and needs no search budget.
- **Overpass never completed a Wyoming pass at full-state scale.** The four quarter-
  state tiles returned zero `historic=aircraft` / `memorial=aircraft` elements, which
  is consistent with OSM simply having almost no aircraft nodes in Wyoming — but a
  tighter Cheyenne bbox query timed out repeatedly and was never retrieved, so the
  Cheyenne gate nodes here came from Nominatim reverse lookups instead. Rhode Island
  returned genuinely zero aircraft nodes statewide.
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" was not consulted.** It would be the right
  check on custody for the Cheyenne F-86 and the Warren UH-1F.
- The Greybull museum's own website was never located, and the Cowboy State Daily
  auction article URL could not be resolved directly — the auction detail here is
  Wikipedia's citation of it.
