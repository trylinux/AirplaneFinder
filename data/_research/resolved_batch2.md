## 5. Douglas C-47B — 43-49942 vs 44-77109 (c/n 16693)
VERDICT: B
CONFIDENCE: high
EVIDENCE: These are two different airframes and one of them no longer exists.
- c/n 16693 = 44-77109, CAF Highland Lakes Squadron "Texas Zephyr", N346AB: built Oklahoma City late 1945, Lend-Lease to Britain, RCAF No. 435 Sqn as KP224, CF serial 12905 from 1970, gifted to the squadron December 2019 and donated to the CAF January 2020. https://www.highlandlakessquadron.com/aircraft/c47
- 43-49942 = N47HL "Bluebonnet Belle", the Highland Lakes Squadron's *previous* C-47, destroyed on takeoff from Burnet Municipal Airport on 21 July 2018 (all 13 aboard survived; airframe burned out). https://commemorativeairforce.org/news/commemorative-air-force-confirms-accident-with-wwii-aircraft-c-47-bluebonnet-belle ; https://www.dailytrib.com/2018/07/21/bluebonnet-belle-crashes-taking-off-from-burnet-airport-all-13-on-board-survive/
So 43-49942 was never at the CAF National Airbase in Dallas as a preserved airframe — it is a 2018 write-off at Burnet, and the two rows were merged on a false identity.
ACTION: keep 6927 (Burnet) as 44-77109 / c/n 16693 "Texas Zephyr"; delete id 6947 (CAF National Airbase Dallas) — 43-49942 is a destroyed airframe, not a preserved one. Do not carry serial 43-49942 onto the Burnet record.

## Cross-cutting notes for the fixer

1. **Hancock Field id 24421 is mistyped.** It is Republic **F-84B 46-600** (ex-Lackland AFB, to New York 1994), not an F-84F, and its c/n field holds the literal string `unknown`. That string is the join key producing conflicts 6, 7 and 10 simultaneously. Fixing the type and nulling the c/n clears three conflicts at once.
2. **Three sites are pure paste-sinks** and each collected several stolen identities: Veterans Memorial Park -- Dixon (conflicts 1, 2), Veterans Park -- Ryan (4, 12, 15) and Veterans Memorial Park -- D'Iberville (3, 14). Worth sweeping the rest of those three sites' inventories for the same failure before trusting any of their rows.
3. **Ryan, Iowa is not a phantom site** — it holds a genuine unidentified AH-1 Cobra. Only the identities pasted onto it are wrong.
4. **Conflict 5 removes an airframe from the catalogue entirely**: C-47B 43-49942 / N47HL "Bluebonnet Belle" burned out at Burnet on 21 July 2018 and is not preserved anywhere.
