Coordinate notes:
- Heritage Park: fix is the HH-1H (OSM node); the park's airframes span 34.8964-34.8985 N / 92.1332-92.1367 W (individual fixes listed per aircraft below). Address is descriptive (OSM places the C-130E on Cannon Drive); postal 72099 = Little Rock AFB.
- Main Gate C-130A: OSM `historic=aircraft` node s/n 56-0518 (AV fix 34.890117/-92.124533 agrees within 20 m). Visitor Center geocodes to 1508 Vandenburg Blvd (OSM spelling) at 34.89049/-92.12455.
- Joint Education Center: OSM unlabeled `historic=aircraft` node "C-130" 220 m east of the JEC building (OSM: "LR1490; Education Center; Starbase" 1490 Vandenburg Blvd at 34.889217/-92.108097) beside US-67/167. Building is outside the security fence and open to the public (ASU-Beebe page).
- Base Ops RF-84F: AV fix; OSM also carries a node "Republic RF-84F Thunderflash s/n 53-7543" at 34.910006/-92.136921 on CMSGT Williams Drive.
- Camp Robinson: fix is the AV F-4C/UH-1V pad (34.8228/-92.2869). Museum building (Lloyd England Hall) geocodes to 34.825531/-92.283613 (OSM). T-37A is 700 m NW at 34.828025/-92.294189 and the F-84F at 34.823553/-92.288286.
- Ebbing collection: fix is the Signature Flight Support building (OSM, 5300 Airport Blvd). The six airframes were towed 11 Jul 2025 to the adjacent Signature ramp for ~3 years while the FMS training centre and a new base entry control point are built; the permanent plan is to line the new entry road inside the fence. Pre-move AV/OSM fixes (row along the 188th Wing entrance) were 35.3385-35.3399 N / 94.3710-94.3714 W with the RF-84F at 35.343064/-94.368922.
- Chaffee: AV fix for the Cobra (35.312356/-94.299425) is 200 m ENE of the Chaffee Barbershop Museum (geocoded 35.311684/-94.301452; 7313 Terry St). Reverse geocode of the fix gives 12160 Redwood Drive; Fort Chaffee 72916.

Access notes:
- LRAFB Heritage Park and Base Ops: inside the controlled gate; military ID or a sponsored visitor pass required.
- Camp Robinson: behind a controlled military gate but the museum page says any visitor can obtain a day pass at the front gate with a driver's licence plus vehicle registration and proof of insurance (Mon-Fri 0800-1500 and the first drill weekend of each month). Recorded as restricted per the BRIEF definition; a one-line description on the site page should say "day pass at gate".
- Ebbing collection: behind the Signature Aviation FBO fence; the wing says the aircraft can be viewed through the fence for the ~3-year interim. Not walk-up.
- Chaffee: the Cobra and an APC sit outdoors beside the museum and are viewable at any time; the indoor Vietnam Veterans Museum itself is by appointment (479-478-0110).

### Corrections / conflicts
- **Jacksonville Museum of Military History (phase 1)**: the aircraft is Republic **F-105F 63-8261** (FY63 F-105F block 63-8260/8366; Baugher; NMUSAF; AV "F-105F c/n F038") — not an F-4. Baugher notes it is falsely marked "6-3261". AV also lists it as "once displayed" at Camp Robinson (it was there before Jacksonville).
- **56-0518 vs 57-0518**: Baugher's 1957 page repeats "now on static display at front gate of Little Rock AFB" under 57-0518 (c/n 3225; MASDC CF013 1976; N9691N; XA-RYZ 1993). The gate aircraft is 56-0518 (c/n 3126; Vietnam evacuation history; AV; OSM; NMUSAF). Treat the 57-0518 note as an error.
- **C-119 manufacturer**: Baugher's block header "Kaiser-Fraser C-119G" for 53-8069/8156 is wrong; Kaiser-built Boxcars were 51-8098/8168. Recorded as Fairchild.
- **OSM C-119 node** carries description "s/n 52-0595" (copy-paste of the B-47 serial). Real serial 53-8084.
- **B-57C 53-3841** formerly painted 53-0521; now correct (Baugher).
- **F-16 at Ebbing** is an F-16A Block 15 (82-0970) though the 2025 newspaper says "F-16C"; **A-10 at Ebbing** is OA-10A/A-10A 77-0216 though the paper says "A-10C".
- **T-37 at Camp Robinson**: NMUSAF lists T-37B; Baugher and AV say T-37A (c/n 40098 is in the T-37A production run). Recorded as A.
- **UH-1 66-15212**: AV says UH-1C; Baugher says converted UH-1M. Recorded as M with UH-1C alias.
- **Camp Robinson Cobra/Huey identities** conflict: Baugher (Oct 2018 sighting) gives AH-1S 71-21023 and UH-1V 73-21718 at an "All Flags Heritage Park"; AV gives AH-1S 68-15129 (stored 2004) and UH-1V 71-20332 (photo Aug 2026). I have recorded 71-21023 / 73-21718 / 71-20332 as separate lines and left 68-15129 out (last evidence 2004 storage). It is possible 68-15129 and 71-21023 are the same airframe under two readings — a data-plate check closes it.
- **RF-101C 56-0057**: AV closes its Camp Robinson display span at "by May 2018" and flags the pad as vacated; Wikipedia still lists it there. Kept as on_display but flagged.
- **F-100A 53-1629 painted serial**: Baugher says displayed 2013 "as 54-629"; AV records original buzz number FW-629. Not verified which is on the airframe now.
