# Arkansas — Phase 3: Monuments and single displays (raw deliverable)

Researched 9 Sep 2026. Scope: VFW / American Legion / VVA posts, city and county parks,
courthouse lawns, airport memorials, school campuses. Museums (Phase 1) and military
installations (Phase 2) are cross-referenced in NOTES only.

Lead-list ancestry used: Aerial Visuals Locator (Arkansas region page, 33 locations
enumerated), Joe Baugher USAF serial pages 1951-1974 (grepped for Arkansas; FY1951-53,
1956-57, 1966-68, 1972 pulled from Wayback copies dated 2023), NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan
by Location" PDF (April 2016), silverhawkauthor "Warplanes of the USA: Arkansas" (Marden
ancestry — lead list only), Wikipedia displayed/surviving lists (T-33, F-101, A-7, AH-1,
UH-1, F-105, F-86, B-47, F-4, T-37). Verification: HMdb marker pages, FAA registry,
local news (5NEWS, Arkansas Democrat-Gazette, Arkansas Living), Flickr dated photos,
post/city web pages.

  Phase-3 location dossier and airframe dossier fetched). Coordinates for every site above

  (curl is 403'd). Arkansas civic loans: T-33A 53-6073 Gravette; T-37B 68-8077 Newport;
  GF-101B 58-0329 Rogers; T-33A 51-8965 West Helena; plus museum/base custody rows.

### 3.2 Corrections / identity judgments
- West Memphis: true identity BuNo 152263 (F-4B-22-MC c/n 0977; later F-4N). Painted
  BuNo 151463 per Aerial Visuals; recorded as alias. Variant recorded as N (as converted
  and displayed); F-4B in aliases.
- Bull Shoals: Baugher and Aerial Visuals conflict on whether 66-15343 survived its 1972
  shoot-down. AV's dated post-1972 depot/rebuild chain is the more specific record, so
  66-15343 is retained as tail_number; Baugher's painted "66-15434" (impossible AH-1G
  serial) is placed in aliases. A photo of the data plate or the painted boom would settle it.
- Rogers Huey 67-17416 c/n 9614: Baugher and AV agree; painted "Headhunters" titles.
- Little Rock "Mother Goose": press calls it UH-1C tail "107"; Baugher/AV/silverhawk
  give 66-15107 converted UH-1M. Recorded as UH-1 | M with UH-1C-BF in aliases.
- year_built populated only where an acceptance/delivery date or FAA YEAR-MFR exists:
  67-17416 (TOS Apr 1968 → 1968); 66-15343 (TOS Dec 1967 → 1967); 66-15107 (purchased
  Apr 1967 per ADG → 1967); N88769 (FAA 1958). All others blank on purpose.
- Site coordinates: Rogers = F-101B pylon (AV site A; Huey is 30 m NW at 36.37073
  -94.10260). Helena = HMdb marker fix. All others = AV site map fix; Newport blank.
