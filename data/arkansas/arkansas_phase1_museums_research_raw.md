### Judgment calls
- Learjet 23 N23BY stays "on_display": FAA registration is live (Younkin family) but the Oct 2025 profile describes it as a static exhibit inside the museum. If it flies again it becomes an airworthy private warbird and drops out.
- Piper J-3 recorded under its historic N20830 because that is how VAN identifies it; the N-number is today on a Cessna 172S — mark as historic marking in description.
- Mystery Ship replica recorded with blank tail_number because FAA N614K belongs to the Tullahoma replica; painted NR614K in aliases.
- Holiday Island / Mountain Home / East Camden included as lines despite no 2023-26 photo because each has an authoritative placement record (Tour of Honor fix; AV placement date; live FAA registration to the college) and nothing suggests removal — flagged for a satellite check.
- Rogers Huey line kept with blank serial: two dated sources (2012 photos; Nov 2023 article) prove presence; identity simply not published.
- Ebbing ANGB six aircraft NOT recorded here (military custody; temporary location) — see cross-references.

- MacArthur Museum of Arkansas Military History, Little Rock — no aircraft/helicopter/missile on any exhibit listing found.
- Museum of Discovery, Little Rock — no aircraft.
- Arkansas Museum of Natural Resources (Smackover) — no aircraft found.
