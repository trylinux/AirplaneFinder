# Pennsylvania — Phase 1: Public civilian museums + discovery sweep
Compiled 10 September 2026. Greenfield state; every line below is a proposed new record.
Pass scope: public civilian museums with aircraft, plus a discovery sweep for holders
that the standard directories miss. Military-base air parks, VFW/Legion posts, city-park
plinths and airport gate guards are NOT enumerated here except where explicitly noted as
hand-offs to the military/monument phases.

## 3. NOTES

### Source weighting used
- Rung 1 (museum's own current pages) carried the collection lists for Mid-Atlantic, Air
  Heritage, Wings of Freedom, American Helicopter Museum, Franklin Institute and Eagles Mere.
- Rung 2 (FAA registry, registry.faa.gov) was used aggressively and productively. The
  **owner-name search** (AircraftInquiry/Search/NameResult?Nametxt=...) turned out to be the
  single most valuable tool in this pass: it independently produced or confirmed the
  registration plus construction number for ~50 Mid-Atlantic airframes, 19 Piper Aviation
  Museum airframes, 19 Golden Age airframes, 9 Air Heritage airframes and 4 Eagles Mere
  airframes, and it is the ONLY hard evidence available for Golden Age's collection. I
  recommend it as standard technique for every remaining state.
- Rung 6/7 (Vintage Aviation News profile; Wikipedia) supplied Wings of Freedom serials that
  the museum's own pages omit, and the Piper and Golden Age type lists.
- Rung 8 lead lists (aviationmuseum.eu, aerocorner) were used ONLY to find sites, and their
  serial tables were discarded — see the KLBE correction below.

### Corrections and conflicts found
1. **aviationmuseum.eu's KLBE Air Museum table is column-shifted**, exactly as the brief
   predicted for their Arkansas tables. It lists registration N302LA against BOTH a Stinson
   AT-19 Reliant and a WSK Lim-2. FAA shows N302LA is a MiG-15UTI/Lim-2. The two other
   registrations it gives for that site (N1918U, NC29767) return NO RECORD in the FAA
   registry. Nothing from that table was used unverified.
2. **Grumman Greenhouse (Jordan Griska), Lenfest Plaza, Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine
   Arts, Philadelphia** — a crushed Grumman S-2/TS-2A Tracker installed as sculpture in 2011.
   It was REMOVED in August 2022. Listed by a current directory as a live PA aviation
   "museum" holding. EXCLUDED: the aircraft has left.
3. **Wings of Freedom "Marine One"** — the H-34 unveiled in October 2025 is the museum's
   existing UH-34J BuNo 145694 repainted as the Eisenhower-era HUS-1, not a second
   helicopter. Directories that add it as a new airframe will double-count.
4. **Wings of Freedom Bell H-13G** — the museum's own page gives "Serial Number 1090" which
   is a Bell construction number; the USAF serial 52-7833 comes from Vintage Aviation News.
   Recorded as 52-7833 with c/n 1090 in aliases.
5. **Franklin Institute T-33** — the museum gives serial 53-6038 and simultaneously describes
   the aircraft as an ex-Navy TO-2/TV-2. A FY1953 USAF serial and a Navy TO-2 identity are
   mutually exclusive. Flagged, not silently resolved.
6. **Mid-Atlantic Fairchild PT-23 "42-47-65"** — the museum's own military-serial cell is
   malformed. Probably 42-4765 but left BLANK rather than guessed.
7. **Mid-Atlantic Pratt-Read TG-32 "315709"** — not a valid USAAF serial in that form; left
   out of tail_number and preserved in aliases.
8. **Mid-Atlantic Meyers OTW** — museum says N46275, FAA says N26475 for the same c/n 28.
   Digits transposed in one source; museum value used with the FAA value in aliases.
9. **Mid-Atlantic Temco D-16 N4487K** — the FAA row gives c/n NAV-4-1487, which is the c/n
   the museum assigns to Navion N91528. One of the two FAA rows is wrong.
10. **Mid-Atlantic Republic "Thunderjet" 46-666** — the museum gives no variant. The FY46
    serial block points to F-84B and I have recorded variant B, but this needs a Baugher
    check before import.
11. **Two HUP-2 Retrievers in Pennsylvania** — American Helicopter Museum (PV-14/HUP-2, BuNo
    unpublished) and Wings of Freedom (HUP-2 BuNo 128517). Almost certainly two different
    airframes, but because one has no BuNo this must be confirmed before import or one will
    be recorded twice.
12. **Piper Aviation Museum PA-23-250 N14230, PA-29 N2900M, PA-31P N9198Y, PA-31T N7500L,
    PA-38 N2476D, PA-41P N9941P** are sourced from Aerial Visuals dossiers and did NOT come
    back in the FAA owner search — registration may have lapsed or transferred. Presence is
    still supported by the museum's own materials.
13. **Piper J3C-65 N70676** appears in the FAA owner search but not on any published list;
    added with a presence caveat.
14. **Air Heritage Beechcraft T-34B/D-45 N15MC** appears in the FAA owner search but has no

- `latitude`/`longitude` are blank for most sites: no mapping or satellite tool was available
  in this pass and the brief forbids guessed coordinates. Street addresses are supplied.
- `year_built` is blank for every military airframe where only a fiscal-year serial was
  available. It is populated for Mid-Atlantic aircraft from the museum's own YEAR column and
  for a handful of others where the source states a build or delivery year in prose.
- `tail_number` is blank wherever a museum publishes no serial (nearly all of the American
  Helicopter Museum, much of Golden Age and Eagles Mere).
- `postal_code` given only where the museum publishes it.
