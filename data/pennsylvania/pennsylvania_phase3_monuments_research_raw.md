### 3.2 Corrections and conflicts resolved

1. **AH-1 67-15663 (c/n 20327) is at Carlisle, not Phillipsburg.** Aerial Visuals lists this one airframe at two PA sites: Phillipsburg (dossier 3569, flagged "airframes not on-site") and the U.S. Army Heritage and Education Center (dossier 10024 site B). OSM 2026 puts an AH-1S at 40.20289,-77.15824, matching the Carlisle fix within ~15 m. **Carlisle is the live location; Phillipsburg is a former one.**
2. **F-94C 51-5671 — custody Corry, location Erie.** NMUSAF (2016) records the loan to VFW Post 264, Corry. The airframe stands at Erie County Memorial Gardens, ~55 km away. Recorded at Erie per the "record where it IS" rule. Sub-conflict unresolved: RoadsideAmerica says it has been at the cemetery since **1959**; Aerial Visuals says it moved from Corry in **November 1993**. Both cannot be right; the 1959 date is more likely a conflation with the cemetery's founding.
3. **Aerial Visuals separately lists an "F-86L" at VFW Post 264, Corry** (dossier 3527, no serial). NMUSAF gives Post 264 only the F-94C. **Not recorded** — see 3.4.
4. **Lycoming County Veterans Memorial Park is in Williamsport, not Jersey Shore.** Aerial Visuals files it under Jersey Shore; the coordinates reverse-geocode to 3407 West Fourth Street, Williamsport 17701. American Legion Post 36 (a separate UH-1) *is* in Jersey Shore borough, ~25 km west. Two distinct sites, both recorded.
5. **"AK-503" is a tail code, not a BuNo.** The Sun-Gazette coverage of the A-6E gives AK-503; the true identity is BuNo 161676 (c/n I-653). Recorded accordingly with AK-503 in aliases.
6. **Post name discrepancy at East Berlin:** OSM says "Richard J. Gross VFW Post 8896", Aerial Visuals says "Richard R. Gross". Went with OSM (surveyed on the ground). Flagged in the record.
7. **VFW Post 7293 is in Whitehall Township (Egypt), not Coplay.** Aerial Visuals files it under "Allentown, Coplay, Cementon / Egypt"; OSM carries the post itself with the address 5209 Spring Mill Road, Whitehall PA 18052.
