## PRIORITY 1 — VFW Post 8168, Midland PA vs Mid-America Air Museum, Liberal KS

### Finding: **there IS a Bell AH-1 Cobra physically on the grounds of VFW Post 8168, Midland, Pennsylvania.**

**Google Street View, "703 PA-68 / Midland Avenue", image capture Jul 2025** shows, head-on and
unambiguously, an olive-drab **AH-1 Cobra** standing on a display pad in the forecourt of a building
whose facade reads **"…W POST 8168"**, with a "Midland VFW Post 8168" banner on the fence beside it.
Identifying features visible in the pano: narrow tandem-seat fuselage with the stepped tandem canopy,
**stub wings**, **chin turret with a twin-gun installation**, two-blade semi-rigid main rotor, and
**"UNITED STATES ARMY"** stencilled on the tail boom. Flags and a planter surround the pad.

Bing aerial at ~30 ft scale independently shows the same airframe from above: a slender fuselage with
a long tail boom, tail rotor and main-rotor blades, on a paved plaza at the corner of 7th Street and
Midland Avenue, immediately in front of the post building. Measured against the scale bar the object
is ~16–17 m nose-to-rotor-tip, consistent with an AH-1 (16.2 m overall).

**Airframe coordinates: 40.63768, -80.45410** (essentially the coordinate already on file,
40.6376194, -80.4541611 — that coordinate is good).

### Mid-America Air Museum, Liberal, Kansas (37.0391, -100.9512)

Bing aerial of the museum shows the large main hangar plus an **outdoor apron/storage row to the
south-west holding roughly six to eight airframes, all of them fixed-wing** (visible wings and
straight fuselages; no rotor, no tail boom, no helicopter silhouette anywhere on the outdoor ramp).
The museum is predominantly an **indoor** collection, so an airframe recorded as *"in storage"* there
would most likely be inside and is **not resolvable from imagery either way**.

### Reasoning on the conflict

- Imagery **positively establishes** that a Cobra is on the ground at Midland PA, in July 2025 — this
  is not a legacy Aerial Visuals claim, it is current.
- Imagery **cannot** confirm the Kansas record, and what *is* visible at Liberal (the outdoor rows)
  contains no Cobra.
- Imagery cannot read a serial. So it does not, by itself, prove the Midland aircraft *is* 71-21031.
  What it does prove is that the airplane.museum entry as it stands — 71-21031 at Mid-America Air
  Museum, Liberal KS, in storage — is the **weaker** of the two records: the Midland location is
  physically verified and current, the Kansas location is unverified and unsupported by what can be
  seen. Recommend moving 71-21031 to VFW Post 8168, Midland PA (marking the serial as
  Aerial-Visuals-sourced / unconfirmed on the airframe), and creating/keeping the Midland site record
  as **PRESENT, verified Jul 2025**.
- Residual risk: the Liberal record could refer to a *different* Cobra whose serial was mis-keyed.
  A serial check on the Midland aircraft (post photos / a visit) is the only thing that closes it.

## Notes on the two-record split at Beaver County Airport

The single shared point (40.7725, -80.3914) is wrong for both records — it sits on the runway
centreline, ~250 m south of anything on display.

- **Air Heritage Aviation Museum** — use **40.7771, -80.3904**. Its aircraft are on the museum's own
  ramp on the north-west side of the field, immediately north-west of the Air Heritage hangar
  (Piper St side). Four airframes are visible outdoors, listed in the table above.
- **Beaver County / F-86H Sabre** — no separate outdoor display could be located anywhere on the
  field. Until it is, this record should be flagged rather than given a coordinate: writing it to the
  Air Heritage ramp point would assert something not observed.

1. Serial confirmation on the Midland Cobra (post photos or a visit) to close the 71-21031 conflict.

   Willow Grove Veterans Memorial Park — all three currently point at unrelated commercial/farm land.
