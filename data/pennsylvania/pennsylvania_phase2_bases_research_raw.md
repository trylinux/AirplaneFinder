## 3. NOTES

### 3a. The Willow Grove disposition question — answer

**Bottom line: the Willow Grove air park did not disperse. It stayed put and became a
public museum.** The stale-data trap here is subtler than the brief anticipated.

- NAS JRB Willow Grove's airfield closed 31 March 2011 and the base closed 15 September
  2011. The Pennsylvania Air National Guard's 111th Attack Wing took custody of the
  cantonment, redesignated Horsham Air Guard Station and now **Biddle Air National Guard
  Base**. The Delaware Valley Historical Aircraft Association's air park and museum —
  the **Harold F. Pitcairn Wings of Freedom Aviation Museum** — remained on its parcel at
  1155 Easton Road, which now sits **outside** the guard perimeter with its own public
  entrance. It is a walk-up ticketed museum today, not a restricted base air park.
  Directories that list "NAS JRB Willow Grove air park, restricted access" are wrong on
  access type but broadly right on the airframes.
- **Per-airframe disposition of the 22 airframes: all 22 remain on the Horsham site.**
  Every aircraft in the section 2 block above was confirmed present in 2025 by two
  independent dated site reports — Photorecon, 22 May 2025 (full walk-through of the
  outdoor air park) and Vintage Aviation News, 30 October 2025 (feature with per-airframe
  serials). Three airframes were noted in May 2025 as away for **off-site** restoration;
  the museum does not name them and the F8U-1 Crusader 143806 is the most likely
  candidate because it is on the museum's own collection page but absent from the May
  2025 outdoor walk-through. Flagged, not dropped.
- **What DID leave Pennsylvania — and left long before 2011.** The famous Willow Grove
  captured-Axis collection (Japanese and German WWII airframes) went to the National
  Museum of Naval Aviation at Pensacola and to the Smithsonian, piecemeal, over decades
  as Pensacola grew — a process the 2025 Vintage Aviation News feature describes as
  finishing well before the base closure. The Aichi M6A1 Seiran and Kugisho MXY7 Ohka
  associated with Willow Grove are Smithsonian NASM accessions. **None of these should be
  recorded in Pennsylvania.**
- **F-14A Tomcat.** Airfields-Freeman's Willow Grove page lists an F-14 among the display
  aircraft, and a Willow Grove F-14 photograph circulates as BuNo 157984. Aerial Visuals'
  dossier for 157984 places it at the National Naval Aviation Museum Pensacola —
  delivered there September 1989 and mounted on the museum's entrance pedestal 10 May
  1990, i.e. *before* the Willow Grove closure. **I do not believe 157984 was the Willow
  Grove display aircraft at the time of closure**; either the photo predates 1989 or the
  identification is wrong. No F-14 is in the Wings of Freedom collection today. This is
  the one genuinely open Willow Grove airframe. Do not create a Pennsylvania F-14 record.
- **Biddle ANGB / 111th Attack Wing gate or heritage display: none found.** The 111th
  converted from A-10s to MQ-9 Reaper ground-control operations and has no flying
  aircraft on station. Searches of the wing's own site, DVIDS and base-beautification
  coverage turned up no gate guard. The A-10A 76-0516 wearing the wing's PA tail code is
  in the museum air park, not at the gate. Recorded as absent, not as unknown.

### 3b. Source hierarchy actually used, and its weight

- Rung 1 (museum's own current pages): wingsoffreedommuseum.org collection and visit
  pages — authoritative on presence, address, hours; publishes **no serials at all**.
  ahec.armywarcollege.edu trail index — authoritative on presence of the Carlisle UH-1;
  no serial.
- Rung 4/6 hybrid, and the strongest currency evidence in this pass: two dated
  independent site reports — Photorecon 22 May 2025 and Vintage Aviation News
  30 October 2025. The latter carries per-airframe serials for ten Wings of Freedom
  airframes, which independently corroborate the directory list.
- Rung 6 (HMdb): the workhorse for Fort Indiantown Gap. Markers 12540, 12542, 12575,
  12580, 12584, 12586, 12588 give marker text, painted serials and coordinate fixes to
  the arc-minute for the EC-130E, both Nikes, the TF-102A, the AH-1F and the UH-1H.
- Base public affairs (rung 1-equivalent for currency): ftig.ng.mil article of
  27 December 2024 on Letterkenny Army Depot restoring the FTIG helicopters — the single
  best currency datum for the Gap, because it proves all three helicopters were physically
  present and being worked on fourteen months before this pass.
- DVIDS: image 6365957, caption dated 14 September 2020, documents the EC-130E being
  relocated *within* Fort Indiantown Gap.
- Rung 4 (Aerial Visuals): dossier 53747 for A-7D 73-1002 — service history, c/n D-398,
  current custody. Dossiers returned **no mapped coordinates** for any PA airframe tried.
- Rung 8 (aviationmuseum.eu) used as a **lead list only** for the Wings of Freedom
  serials; nine of the twenty-two serials rest on it alone and are flagged as such above.
- Rung 7 (Wikipedia survivor lists): the A-7 survivors list produced 73-1002 at the 171st
  ARW; the displayed-UH-1 list produced **zero** military-base entries in Pennsylvania.

### 3c. Corrections made

1. **Access type of the Willow Grove air park.** Directories carry it as a restricted
   base air park. It is a public ticketed museum with a civilian street entrance and has
   been since the 2011 base closure. Corrected.
2. **TF-102A not F-102A at Fort Indiantown Gap.** The HMdb marker is headed "F-102 Jet
   Fighter" and the marker text describes the single-seat F-102A, but the marker itself
   carries a note that the exhibit is a two-seat TF-102A operational trainer. Serial
   56-2346 sits inside the TF-102A block, confirming the two-seater. Recorded as
   variant TF-102A.
3. **One EC-130E, two markers.** HMdb 12540 ("Commando Solo") and 12542 ("Commando Solo
   I") both describe serial 63-7773. Directories that count these as two airframes are
   wrong. One record created.
4. **The Willow Grove Axis aircraft are not Pennsylvania records.** They left decades
   before the closure. Excluded explicitly so nobody re-researches them.
5. **F-14A 157984 is in Florida, not Pennsylvania.** See 3a.

### 3e. Deliberately blank fields

- **year_built**: blank everywhere except EC-130E 63-7773 (1963), where a DVIDS caption

  inference. No other airframe in this pass has a sourced construction or delivery date.
  FY prefixes were **not** converted into build years anywhere.
- **tail_number**: blank for the Carlisle UH-1, the Wings of Freedom QH-50C and the two

### 3f. Targets checked and EXCLUDED — do not re-research these

- **Biddle Air National Guard Base / Horsham Air Guard Station (111th Attack Wing)** —
  no gate guard, no heritage display. MQ-9 ground-control unit with no aircraft on
  station. The wing's own site, DVIDS coverage and base-beautification reporting show
  nothing.

- **193rd Special Operations Wing, Harrisburg IAP / Middletown** — **no display on the

  photographically by NHHC but nothing is preserved on site.
- **Letterkenny Army Depot (Chambersburg)** — Letterkenny *restores* display aircraft for
