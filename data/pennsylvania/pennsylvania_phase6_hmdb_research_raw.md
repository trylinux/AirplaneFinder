### 3.2 Sources used and their weight

- **HMdb.org marker pages** — rung 6. Give an exact coordinate, on-site
  photography, an erection date and a dated catalogue/revision stamp. Used as
  the primary currency evidence for the one new record. HMdb's catalogue date
  is a genuine dated re-inspection of the listing but is *not* proof the
  photograph is that recent, so it was paired with a second source.
- **AOPA news (aopa.org), 16 November 2020** — rung 6-equivalent trade press;
  source of the Cessna 310F model designation and 1961 build year.
- **EAA / Hangar Flying (inspire.eaa.org), 20 October 2020** — corroborates the
  restoration and dedication.
- **FAA registry** — checked for N6775X; returns "Deregistered" only, with no
  YEAR MFR or serial exposed. Year built therefore rests on AOPA, not FAA.
- **Wikipedia survivor lists** (F-84 Thunderjets, F-86 Sabres, displayed UH-1)
  — rung 7, used only as a cross-check. Their Pennsylvania entries were
  46-0666 (Mid-Atlantic Air Museum), 53-1338 (Beaver County Airport) and
  TH-1L 157817 (American Helicopter Museum) — **all three already recorded in
  phases 1 and 3**. No new airframe came from them.

### 3.3 Corrections and judgment calls

- **HMdb marker 288368 titles the airframe only "Cessna 310".** The marker text
  gives registration N6775X and "Serial #0075" and a November 1961 purchase.
  The variant **F** and the 1961 build year come from AOPA, not from the
  marker; the alias is recorded as `c/n 0075` exactly as the marker states
  rather than expanded to the conventional Cessna form `310F0075`, because that
  expansion is an inference.
- **This is a civil aircraft, not military.** Stewart's USAF rank is biography,
  not the airframe's service history; `military_civilian` = civilian and
  `role_type` = private.
- **Not a replica.** Multiple independent 2015-2020 accounts trace the actual
  airframe from Dallas Executive Airport to Indiana PA.

### 3.6 Serial / block check

- **N6775X** is a civil registration, not a military serial, so no production
  block applies. The FAA registry no longer exposes the record (deregistered),
  so the registration could not be independently re-verified against a current
  FAA record; it is attested by the marker inscription, by AOPA and by EAA, all

  c/n "0075" is from the marker only and is single-sourced.

### 3.7 Method finding — for whoever runs the next pass

The town/county HMdb sweep **works mechanically but has a low yield in
Pennsylvania**, and this needs recording so it is not re-run at full cost:

- HMdb's `Search=County&County=<X>+County&State=Pennsylvania` is genuinely
  state-filtered and reliable. `Search=Place&Town=<x>&State=Pennsylvania` is
  too.
- HMdb's **topic browse is NOT state-filterable**. `Results.asp?Search=Topic&
  CategoryID=51` (Air & Space) returns 5492 entries nationally, ignores
  `State=`, ignores `StartAt=`, and its `Page=` parameter returns a JavaScript
  "processing your request" interstitial to a fetcher. There is no cheap way to
  get "Air & Space markers in Pennsylvania" as a single list. Do not spend
  budget retrying this.
- Direct `curl` of hmdb.org is blocked by a Cloudflare managed challenge.
  A rendering fetcher is required. HMdb also rate-limits: roughly three
  concurrent fetches is the ceiling before 429s, and a ~20-30 s pause clears it.
- Counties with more than 100 markers paginate at 100 and a fetcher summarising
  page 1 will silently miss later pages. **Confirmed miss:** the Monroe County
  listing summary did not surface the Tobyhanna Army Depot aircraft markers
  that are demonstrably in that county. Treat any single-page county summary as
  a floor, not a census.
- Net result of the whole sweep: **one new site, one new airframe.** The four
  earlier passes had already absorbed essentially all of Pennsylvania's
  HMdb-documented aircraft displays. The remaining Pennsylvania gap is almost
  certainly in displays that carry **no HMdb marker at all** — Legion/VFW post
  airframes that were never submitted to the database.

4. **Cessna 310 N6775X c/n.** The marker's "Serial #0075" is single-sourced and

   pair the VFW Department of Pennsylvania post directory (vfwpa.org) and the
   American Legion Department of Pennsylvania roster against satellite imagery,
   post by post. Nothing in this pass changes that conclusion — it reinforces it.
