## 3. NOTES

### Sources and the weight given to each

- **Museum's own current pages (rung 1)** carried the collection lists for the Glenn H.
  Curtiss Museum (exhibits page, fetched 10 Sep 2026), the National Warplane Museum
  (collection index and per-type pages), the National Soaring Museum (its own 77-row
  glider table with registrations), the Wings of Eagles Discovery Center (its own
  current aircraft table), the Niagara Aerospace Museum, the Buffalo Naval Park, the
  Rochester airport exhibits page, the Plattsburgh AFB Museum and the History Center in
  Tompkins County. These are the backbone of this pass.
- **FAA registry (rung 2)** — the online owner-name search at
  registry.faa.gov/AircraftInquiry/Search/NameResult is currently BROKEN: every query,
  including single words with no punctuation, redirects to
  `NameInquiry?...&error=Name%20has%20unsupported%20characters`. Workaround used and
  recommended for the remaining passes: download
  `https://registry.faa.gov/database/ReleasableAircraft.zip` (73 MB, dated 9 Sep 2026)
  and join MASTER.txt to ACFTREF.txt locally. That gave exact owner-name and
  city-level sweeps for the whole state in seconds and is far higher-yield than the web
  form ever was.
- **US Army display-helicopter loan list** (warbirdsandairshows.com/armydisplayhelicopters.htm)
  was the single biggest discovery source for monument-class sites in this pass — it
  produced fourteen upstate VFW/Legion/MCL/AMVETS sites that appear on no museum list.
- **Wikipedia survivor lists** (F-101, A-4, AH-1) used for corroboration only.
- **Aerial Visuals** used for dossiers on individual serials.
- **silverhawkauthor** and **aviationmuseum.eu** used strictly as lead lists; several of
  their claims were falsified (below) and every surviving line says which rung it rests on.
- The NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" PDF at nationalmuseum.af.mil returned a
  477-byte stub rather than the document; not usable this pass.

### Corrections and falsified leads

- **silverhawkauthor's Wings of Eagles entry is ~20 years stale.** It lists roughly 40
  airframes at Horseheads including a PBY-6A, a P-40E, a C-46F, an FH-1 Phantom, an
  F2H-2P Banshee, a MiG-21, a C-119G, an A-37B, an AT-11 and an He 162 replica. The
  museum's own current aircraft page lists 15 items. FAA checks confirm the dispersal:

### Judgment calls

- **Geneseo airworthy fleet.** The National Warplane Museum and the 1941 Historical
  Aircraft Group are the **same organisation at the same site** — the FAA registrations
  for the museum's aircraft are held under "1941 Historical Aircraft Group Museum",
  "1941 Aircraft Group", "Grasshopper Aviation Inc" and "Ashworth Corp Inc", all at
  Geneseo. I recorded them as one site, not two. The whole fleet is hangared at Geneseo
  and shown to paying visitors year-round, including the airworthy aircraft, so I
  counted the flyers (Whiskey 7, the Staggerwing, the Cornell, the BT-13, the Fokker
  replicas, the Champs) as displays. Airworthy warbirds merely *based* at Geneseo but
  privately owned (e.g. N9123N Genesee Warbirds HA-200, N58698 Valiant Air Group BT-13
  at Hamburg, N119H P-51D at Amsterdam) are **excluded** as operational private aircraft.
- **National Soaring Museum display_status.** All 77 are recorded `on_display`. The
  museum publishes a single undivided collection list and does not say which are in
  storage; its Harris Hill hall clearly cannot show 77 at once. Treat this field as the
  weakest in the NSM block — see open questions.
- **Replicas** are recorded (per the brief) and flagged in `description`, never in
  `aliases`: the Curtiss Museum's nine reproductions, the Rochester airport Taylor
  Chummy and Curtiss Pusher, the Niagara Curtiss Pusher and Jenny, the NSM Wright
  gliders, the Geneseo Fokkers and L-21B.
- **Link trainers excluded.** Geneseo's "AN-T-18 Link Trainer" and the Curtiss Museum's
  Link trainer are ground trainers, not airframes.
- **Bell Rocket Belt, Lunar Module ascent engine and the Apollo mission control panel**
  at Niagara Aerospace are components, not airframes. Excluded.

### Deliberately blank fields

- `year_built` is blank on every military airframe where I have only a fiscal-year
  serial prefix. It is populated only from FAA YEAR-MFR or a museum's own build-year label.
- `tail_number` blank: Curtiss C-46; Curtiss P-40E restoration and the ¾-scale
  reproduction; all Curtiss reproductions; Geneseo BT-13A, SNV-2, AT-6A, B-23, C-47B
  Gooneybird, CT-133, both Fokkers, the second Champ; Wings of Eagles TBM-3E, J-4A,
  Breezy, Whirlwind; Niagara P-39 and Cunningham-Hall; Tonawanda F9F-6P; Sampson T-33A
  and T-2C; Eden AH-1F; EADS F-4; all eight Dart Airport aircraft.
- `latitude`/`longitude` blank on all VFW/Legion/MCL/AMVETS posts and on the Oriskany
  Museum — I have no airframe fix or street address for them and a town centroid would
  be a guess. Dart Airport's coordinates are the airport reference point, not an
  airframe fix; every other coordinate is either an HMdb/Wikipedia airframe fix
  (Plattsburgh FB-111A, Sampson T-2C, Griffiss B-52, Tonawanda F9F) or a geocoded
  street address.
- `postal_code` given only where the site publishes an address.
