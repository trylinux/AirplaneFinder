## 3. NOTES

### Sources used and their weight
- **OpenStreetMap Overpass (overpass.private.coffee, 2026 extract)** — highest-value CURRENCY source in this
  pass. Queried historic=aircraft, memorial=aircraft and memorial:type=aircraft as nodes and ways over
  40.4,-79.8,45.1,-71.8. 102 elements returned but the bounding box spills into Ontario, Quebec-border
  Vermont, western Massachusetts, Connecticut, New Jersey and north-western Pennsylvania; only about 25
  elements are actually in New York. Every NY node was reverse-geocoded through Nominatim. OSM confirmed
  present-in-2026: Sampson State Park (2 nodes), DeRuyter AL 894, Perinton Freedom Hill, Walter M. Kenney
  Park Tonawanda, Westchester County Airport, Wheatfield area cluster, Bethpage F-14, and the Big Flats
  cluster. OSM tag coverage of New York monuments is far thinner than Pennsylvania's — most posts are absent.
- **Aerial Visuals Locator (POST Region=New York, Scope=3)** — the workhorse IDENTITY source. 149 New York
  location rows returned. Each monument dossier gives an arc-second coordinate on the airframe plus serial
  and c/n. Vintage is roughly 2008-2015 and it is NOT a currency source; it also routinely mislabels US Army
  Hueys and Cobras as USAF, which is corrected silently in the descriptions above.
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" (updated April 2016)** — retrieved via
  web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/ of aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf and read with pdftotext. The
  2023id_ path served an HTML wrapper, not the PDF. Authoritative on USAF civic-loan CUSTODY. Confirmed
  Moira AT-38B 61-0806, Central Square F-86H 53-1306, East Aurora F-84F 51-1748, Monroe F-86L 52-10052,
  Sampson T-33A 51-8604 and Rome B-52G 58-0225 (Legion Post 24).
- **HMdb.org** — Cloudflare blocks curl entirely; reachable only through WebFetch. The keyword search for
  New York + helicopter returned dedicated markers for East Aurora, Eden (with serial 67-15506), Gowanda
  (with serial 67-17611) and Carmel. Marker 270952 for the City of Tonawanda Cobra is photographed and
  catalogued 22 April 2025 — the single best currency datum in this pass.
- **Wikipedia survivor lists** (F-4, F-86, F-84, F-100, F-101, T-33, A-4, F-105) grepped for New York. Thin
  but produced the one really valuable correction: Central Square's F-86H is painted as 53-0915.
- **silverhawkauthor.com New York page (Bethpage to Woodridge)** — LEAD LIST only, and it shares Andy
  Marden ancestry with Aerial Visuals so agreement between the two is NOT two sources. It nonetheless
  produced one site Aerial Visuals misses entirely (VFW Post 369, Mexico) and the dated 15 June 2024
  photograph of the East Aurora F-84F.
- **Local news** (Lockport Union-Sun & Journal, Niagara Gazette, Orleans Hub, Buffalo News, WGRZ) — the only
  way to find post-2015 installations. Produced the Hartland/Gasport Huey and the 2016 move of the
  Tonawanda Cobra.

### Corrections made
- **Central Square, American Legion Post 915** — the F-86H wears 53-0915 but the true identity is 53-1306.
  Recorded 53-1306 in tail_number and 53-0915 in aliases.
- **AH-1F 67-15690** — Aerial Visuals and silverhawkauthor place this Cobra on the roof of the Niagara
  Aerospace Museum entrance in Niagara Falls. It is not there. It was loaned by the museum to Vietnam
  Veterans of America Chapter 77 and installed in the City of Tonawanda's Veterans Park; HMdb marker 270952
  (April 2025) fixes it at 43 00.644 N 78 54.258 W. Recorded at Tonawanda only.
- **Adams vs Henderson** — Aerial Visuals closed record 3127 notes that the AH-1 attributed to American
  Legion Post 586 in Adams is actually at the Alexander Corners memorial in Henderson, sponsored by that
  post. Recorded at Henderson. Do not re-research Adams.
- **Two Tonawandas** — the Grumman F9F-6P is at Walter M. Kenney Park in the TOWN of Tonawanda (Brighton
  Road at Colvin Boulevard, Erie County); the AH-1F is in Veterans Park in the CITY of Tonawanda (Niagara
  County). These are different municipalities and different sites; several directories conflate them.
- **Perinton post number** — the Freedom Hill Huey is variously attributed to American Legion Post 8495 and
  to VFW Perinton Memorial Post 8495. Post 8495 is a VFW post; the site name above follows Aerial Visuals'
  spelling but the affiliation should be corrected to VFW before publication.
- Aerial Visuals lists numerous UH-1/AH-1 airframes as "USAF" that are unambiguously US Army by serial
  block (Cuba, Lyons, Wheatfield, Waterloo, East Farmingdale). Corrected in description text.

### Serials that FAILED or half-failed a production-block check — flagged, not dropped
- **American Legion Post 79, Massena — "UH-1V 66-0831 c/n 5314".** FY66 US Army Huey serials run
  66-15000 to 66-17138. 66-0831 belongs to no UH-1 block found. The c/n 5314 is a plausible Bell 205 number,
  so the airframe is real and the serial is probably mis-transcribed. Needs a data-plate or plaque reading.
- **The Oriskany Museum at Trinkaus Park — "A-4E / NA-4E 148613".** 148613 does not fall inside any
  published A-4E BuNo block. Either the BuNo or the variant is wrong. Recorded as given with the doubt
  stated.
- **Westchester County Airport T-33.** Two sources give different identities against the SAME c/n 580-9594
  (T-33B BuNo 141538 vs T-33A 55-3053). One is a mis-transcription. 141538 preferred because it sits
  correctly in the T-33B BuNo range and carries an FAA N-number (N13007) that can settle it in one lookup.

### Judgment calls
- **Sampson World War II Navy Veterans Memorial Museum** is included here because both airframes stand on
  open pylons in a New York state park and are reachable without entering the museum building. If the museum
  pass claims it, this entry should be merged, not duplicated.
- **Fulton County Airport XA3D-1 125413** is recorded as in_storage rather than on_display: the A-3
  Skywarrior Association's own display list does not carry it, which for a prototype of that significance is
  a strong negative signal. Presence is genuinely unconfirmed.
- **77 Water Street Sopwith Camel** is a replica and is flagged as a replica in description, never in
  aliases, per the brief. access_type is restricted because it is a rooftop visible only from adjacent towers.
- **School and college campuses** (Vaughn, Aviation High School, Wilson Tech) are recorded with
  access_type = appointment. The airframes are deliberately retained and presented, but a member of the
  public cannot walk up to them.
- **Museum of Modern Art Bell 47** is recorded: it is a single deliberately-presented airframe, even though
  the institution is an art museum rather than an aviation one.

### Deliberately blank fields
- **year_built is blank on every line.** No sourced construction, delivery or acceptance date was found for
  any airframe in this pass, and no FY prefix was converted into a year.
- postal_code is blank for VFW Post 369 Mexico and Hartland Veterans Memorial (street address not fixed).
- latitude/longitude blank for VFW Post 369 Mexico and Hartland Veterans Memorial — no fix on the airframe
  exists in any source consulted and the brief forbids guessing.
- BuNo blank for the Tonawanda F9F-6P and the Arnot Mall SGS 1-26; registration blank for the MoMA Bell 47;
  serial blank for the Hartland Huey.
