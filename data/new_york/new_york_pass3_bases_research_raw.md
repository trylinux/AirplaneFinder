## NOTES

### Sources used and their weight
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" PDF, updated April 2016** (rung 5) — the single most productive source for this pass. The live URL is 403 to curl; retrieved instead from the Wayback Machine at `web.archive.org/web/2018id_/http://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Portals/7/documents/other/aircraft_on_loan_by_location.pdf` and read with pdftotext. It yields 48 New York rows; the base/ANG rows are reproduced above. It is authoritative on *custody* only and is ten years stale on presence.
- **Joe Baugher serial pages via the crouze.com mirror** (rung 3) — decisive for true-vs-painted identity on four airframes (C-47 43-48957, F-4B 151021, F-16A 79-0337, F-94B/YF-97A 50-0877). The USN third-series pages 17-20 are 404 on joebaugher.com and absent from crouze; retrieved via `web.archive.org/web/2023id_/https://www.joebaugher.com/navy_serials/thirdseries19.html`.
- **Aerial Visuals dossiers** (rung 4) — confirmed "latest owner or location" for 12 airframes. Note their search page renders server-side, so `Airframes.php?Seeds=<serial>` works with plain curl even though the results look empty after naive tag-stripping.
- **DVIDS / af.mil / ang.af.mil / dmna.ny.gov** (rung 1-equivalent for base displays) — dated currency: Niagara air-park photos 8 Jun 2022 and article 24 Oct 2024; Stratton LC-130 restoration 15 Jul 2026; Gabreski gate guardian 17 Aug 2021; Latham F-16 7 Nov 2015; EADS F-4 10 Dec 2010.
- **HMdb.org** (rung 6) — marker text and coordinates for the Niagara C-119 (m=75901, m=75929) and the Plattsburgh FB-111A (m=92744).
- **OpenStreetMap via overpass.private.coffee** — a statewide `historic=aircraft` bbox query returned 107 nodes. Six cluster at Hancock Field's East Molloy Road entrance (43.1002-43.1009 N, 76.1031/76.1068-76.1072 W), exactly matching the six NMUSAF-listed Syracuse airframes; four cluster at Niagara Falls ARS. OSM has **nothing** at Plattsburgh, Scotia, Latham, Newburgh, Westhampton Beach or Rome, so those sites got no OSM coordinate fix.
- Rome Sentinel / Spectrum News / WKTV / Oneida County (local press, rung 6) for the B-52 tornado damage and repair timeline.

### Corrections with evidence
1. **Niagara C-119 is not 51-2680.** The airframe is ex-RCAF 22105 / ex-USAF 51-8101 / ex-N15506, c/n 10736. It is *painted* as 51-2680, a 440th TCW C-119G lost near Grand Turk 5 June 1965. Evidence: HMdb marker text at the aircraft plus the Aerial Visuals dossier (Serial=18568) which states "Displayed in these markings … 12680/680".
2. **Niagara C-47 wears 43-15751, not its own serial.** Baugher's BuNo entry for 150190: "ex USAF 43-48957 … Returned to USAF as 43-48957, now on loan to Niagra Falls, NY Heritage Park as 43-15751." Aerial Visuals dossier 58919 confirms the 150190/43-48957 identity, c/n 14773.
3. **EADS Rome "F-4D 66-7456" is really F-4B BuNo 151021.** Baugher, USN third series: "The plane marked as 66-7456 on display at Eastern Air Defense Sector building … is actually Navy F-4B 151021 which got converted to look like a F-4E for test purposes. She is painted as F-4D 66-7456 in 107 FIG / 136 FIS NY ANG colors." The 2010 DMNA press release never gives a serial — it only says the aircraft came from the Newport NY radar test site.
4. **Hancock Field F-16A 79-0337 is painted 80-0205.** Baugher FY79: "to BR training at Syracuse NY ANG. Reported Jun 2006 stored at Syracuse AFB, New York, painted as 80-0205."
5. **Hancock Field "F-94B 50-0877" is the YF-97A / YF-94C prototype.** Baugher FY50 lists 50-877 as a one-off: "Lockheed YF-97 Starfire … modified from F-94B airframe on the production line. First flight Jan 19, 1950. Later redesignated YF-94C. On display with New York ANG at Syracuse." The NMUSAF loan list's "F-94B" is wrong. This is a significant airframe and should not be catalogued as an ordinary F-94B.
6. **Aerial Visuals calls Syracuse's 56-3493 an "A-37 Dragonfly".** Baugher gives it as T-37B, MSN 40065, to AMARC 1991 and back to the 174th FW in 2000. T-37B is correct; A-37 is an Aerial Visuals data error.
7. **F-16A 80-0504 has moved.** The April 2016 NMUSAF list still charges it to NY ANG Scotia (109 AW), but the DMNA press release of Nov 2015 and Aerial Visuals dossier 35519 both place it at the New York National Guard headquarters gate in Latham, where it was erected 7 Nov 2015. It is recorded at Latham, not at Stratton.
8. **Rome B-52G 58-0225 is not simply "on display".** An EF-2 tornado in July 2024 lifted the 170000-pound aircraft off its pedestal. Oneida County allocated 150000 dollars in Feb 2025 (Rome Sentinel / WKTV / Oneida County release) and Spectrum News on 14 July 2025 reported it back on the pedestal "but work continues". Recorded as `under_restoration`; a 2026 recheck is warranted before flipping to `on_display`.
9. **Plattsburgh B-47E is 53-2385, not 52-0166.** 52-0166 is at Castle Air Museum, California, per the same NMUSAF loan list. Baugher FY53 names 53-2385 "Pride of the Adirondacks" at the former Plattsburgh AFB.

### Judgment calls
- **Access type.** Niagara Falls ARS, Hancock Field, Stratton ANGB, Stewart ANGB and EADS Rome are `restricted` — everything is inside a controlled gate. Niagara runs escorted base tours 1 April to 30 September with four weeks' notice and a background check, which is still restricted, not appointment-class public access. Gabreski is marked `public` because both gate guardians sit at the Old Riverhead Road front-gate apron and are viewable and photographable from the public road; same reasoning for the Latham F-16 and Cobra at the armory gate on Old Niskayuna Road, and for the Rome B-52 and both Plattsburgh airframes, which stand in open business-park / former-base ground.
- **The Fort Drum "Black Hawk" is EXCLUDED.** The DVIDS article of 21 Nov 2025 makes clear it is a mock-up built by Fort Drum DPW in seven weeks from wood, aluminium and foam around a frame piece bought from the production of the film *The Day After Tomorrow*, suspended in the Heritage Center foyer. It is not an airframe.
- **The Niagara C-130H is recorded with a blank tail_number.** The base's own 2018 and 2022 articles, the DVIDS captions and the 2024 air-park article all avoid a serial; the aircraft wears the symbolic marking "914-1". Blank beats a guess.
- **The Latham AH-1 is recorded with a blank tail_number** for the same reason — the DMNA release names the type only.
- **F-84B 46-600 is listed but flagged.** Baugher bounds the Syracuse display at "1994 to 2002" while the 2016 NMUSAF list still charges it to 174 MXG/MXA and OSM shows six aircraft at the base entrance, which is exactly the NMUSAF count including 46-600. Two readings are possible: either Baugher's end-date is an error, or one of the six OSM nodes is a different airframe. Ranked first among open questions.

### EXCLUDED sites and airframes — do not re-research
- **United States Military Academy, West Point** (the Plain, Trophy Point, the West Point Museum, Buffalo Soldier Field, Fort Putnam): **no aircraft, helicopter, missile or rocket on display.** Trophy Point holds captured cannon and the Battle Monument. The West Point Museum's large ordnance holdings are a *Fat Man* atomic bomb casing (a bomb, not a missile or rocket, and a display model) plus small arms and artillery. New York Army Guard helicopters appear at West Point only for periodic flying demonstrations. No record.
- **Fort Drum / 10th Mountain Division and Fort Drum Museum, Watertown**: the museum (Building 2509, Col. Reade Road, phone 315-774-0391) is an indoor narrative museum with no aircraft; the Heritage Center Black Hawk is a film-prop mock-up; the Black Hawk Training Crash Memorial is a plaque with eleven flowering pear trees and *a picture* of a Black Hawk, no airframe. **No record at Fort Drum.**
- **Watervliet Arsenal** — the arsenal museum is a cannon and gun-tube collection; no aircraft or missiles found.
- **Fort Hamilton, Brooklyn** (Harbor Defense Museum), **Fort Totten, Queens**, **Fort Wadsworth, Staten Island** — coast-artillery fortification sites; no aircraft or displayed missiles found.
- **Camp Smith, Peekskill** — no displayed airframe found; the NY DMNA "Requesting a National Guard Aircraft (Static) Display" page covers *temporary* flyaway appearances by operational aircraft, which are not records.
- **Former Roslyn Air Force Station / Roslyn ANGS, East Hills** — demolished 2005 and replaced by the Park at East Hills; only derelict buildings in the woods remain. Nothing preserved.
- **Former Mitchel Field, Garden City — base side** — the surviving Mitchel airframes are inside the Cradle of Aviation Museum (A-10A 76-0535, F-105B 57-5783, P-47N 44-89444 among them, per the NMUSAF list). There is no separate base-side display. Cradle of Aviation belongs to another pass.
- **Former Suffolk County AFB, Westhampton** — the site is today Gabreski Airport / Gabreski ANGB and its displays are the two gate guardians recorded above. No separate former-base display.
- **US Merchant Marine Academy, Kings Point** — the American Merchant Marine Museum is a maritime museum; no aircraft found.
- **US Coast Guard in New York** — no preserved Coast Guard airframe found in the state. Air Station Brooklyn (Floyd Bennett Field) closed in 1998 and its aircraft dispersed; the HH-52A at Intrepid is a museum holding, not a Coast Guard base display. Air Station Cape Cod is Massachusetts and out of scope.
- **Youngstown / Old Fort Niagara** — 18th-century fortification; no aircraft. (Youngstown Air Reserve Station is in Ohio, not New York — a common confusion.)
- **New York Nike sites** — New York had 18 Nike sites in two defence areas (NY-01 to NY-99 around the city, and the Niagara-Buffalo NF/BU sites). The only surviving structures are NY-49 Fort Tilden (NPS) and NY-03/04 Orangeburg. **No displayed Nike Ajax or Hercules was found at any former New York Nike site.** The two Nike Hercules in New York are (a) Buffalo Naval and Servicemen's Park, dockside, on its launcher beside USS Little Rock — a museum holding for another pass, and (b) a Nike Hercules training round at a privately owned armour museum at Mattituck on the North Fork, fenced and reported by ed-thelen.org as "never seems to be open" — recommend excluding unless someone can confirm public or appointment access. Sandy Hook NY-56 is in New Jersey. The Niagara base's 24 Oct 2024 article lists a Nike missile display as a *planned* future air-park addition, not an existing one.
- **AFROTC/AROTC campus displays** — a scan of New York AFROTC detachments (Clarkson Det 536, RIT, SUNY Potsdam, NYU, St John's) turned up no campus airframe. Not exhaustively checked; see open questions.
- **Planned but not present at Niagara**: a *scale model* KC-135 for the air park (a model, not an airframe) and a Nike missile display, both announced Oct 2024.

### Deliberately blank fields
- `year_built` is blank on every line except the Niagara C-119 (HMdb marker: "manufactured in 1953 by Fairchild Aircraft in Hagerstown, Maryland"). No other airframe here has a sourced construction, delivery or acceptance date. Fiscal-year prefixes were not converted.
- `tail_number` blank: Niagara C-130H; Latham AH-1.
- `latitude`/`longitude` blank at Stratton, Latham, Stewart, Gabreski and EADS Rome — no airframe-level fix was obtainable (no OSM node, no HMdb marker, no Aerial Visuals map coordinate). Street addresses are given so these can be reverse-geocoded.
- `postal_code` given only where the base's published mailing ZIP is unambiguous.
- The Rome B-52 coordinate 43.21628 / -75.40650 comes from an *untagged* OpenStreetMap `historic=aircraft` node in Griffiss park and is the only large airframe mapped there; treat as probable, not certain.
- The Plattsburgh coordinate is the HMdb fix on the FB-111A marker. The B-47 sits in the same former-base air-park complex but may be up to several hundred metres away; a separate fix would improve the record.

### Open questions, ranked
1. **Is Republic F-84B 46-600 still at Hancock Field?** Baugher bounds it 1994-2002; NMUSAF 2016 says yes. One question settles it.
2. **What is the true USAF serial of the Niagara Falls C-130H marked "914-1"?** It came from the Connecticut ANG at Bradley Field in Sept 2017 having finished service at Savannah ANGB, Georgia. The 914th ARW historian will know.
3. **Are F-100D 56-2993 and F-4C 64-0660 still on the Niagara Falls installation?** Neither appears in 2022-2024 air-park imagery; they were charged to the 107th's side of the base, not to the 914th's air park. 64-0660 is a triple MiG-killer and worth pinning down.
4. **Is Stewart's O-2A 67-21376 still displayed?** Last dated image is 5 Oct 2001; only the 2016 loan list supports it since.
5. **Is Rome's B-52G 58-0225 fully repaired as of 2026?** July 2025 said back on pedestal, work continuing.
6. **What is the serial of the AH-1 Cobra at the Latham headquarters gate?**
7. **Exact coordinates for the Stratton, Stewart, Gabreski, EADS and B-47 airframes.**
8. **Is the Mattituck Nike Hercules publicly viewable at all?** If not, it stays excluded.
9. **Any NY armory with a helicopter on a pad other than Latham?** Rochester, Buffalo, Binghamton and Ronkonkoma were searched and produced nothing; the search was not exhaustive.

### Not reached in this pass
Nothing in the target list was left unexamined, but three areas got only a first-pass sweep rather than an exhaustive one: statewide National Guard armory helicopter pads, university ROTC detachment displays, and any former Nike site with a re-erected missile that is not indexed by ed-thelen.org or themilitarystandard.com. All three produced zero positives on the searches run.
