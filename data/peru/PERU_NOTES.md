# PERU — research notes (all three passes: museums, base collections, monuments)

Output directory: `/home/claude/sa/peru/`. All CSVs are generated by `python3 build_peru.py`
from `peru_data.py` (edit the data file, re-run; the script enforces the enum vocabularies,
adds dashless alias forms and checks the alias rules). `pe_museums.csv` + one
`<slug>_aircraft.csv` per site.

## 1. How this was researched, and what could not be reached

The session lost WebSearch after ~15 queries (budget shared with parallel agents) and the
sandbox blocks airhistory.net, jetphotos, airfighters, key.aero, scramble.nl, planespotters,
Facebook/Instagram, Google/Bing/DDG results pages and the Wayback Machine. What *did* work,
and was used, in order of weight:

1. **Dated photographs on Flickr** (search + individual photo pages, and the images themselves
   were downloaded and inspected). Three photographers gave the backbone of the currency
   evidence: `105285627@N06` (Lima/Callao/Pisco, 9-11 Aug 2023, captions say "preserved at …"),
   `chrischenn76` (Parque del Aire, 31 Jul 2022), `namcys11` (Nov 2014, Nov 2018, Nov 2023),
   `158949556@N05` (Las Palmas, 13-14 Mar 2024), plus `l8rmt`/`markp51` (Nov 2014) and
   `santiagostucchi` (Parque Temático FAP, Jul 2019).
2. **abpic.co.uk** (Air-Britain) location listing for "Las Palmas AB (SPLP)" — 1997-2010 photos
   with serials (older baseline, used for provenance, not currency).
3. **aerialvisuals.ca Locator** — Peru location list with coordinates and serials (undated;
   treated as leads, several of its entries are storage/dump hulks, not displays).
4. **OpenStreetMap/Nominatim** — memorial nodes for Parque del Aire airframes, MB-339 486 in
   Surco, the Blériot replica at Las Palmas, the Pachacámac Mi-8, and park names
   ("Parque del Avión" ×3, "Parque El Avión" ×3, "Parque Aeronáutico de Castilla").
5. **YouTube search results + thumbnails** (titles/dates only; video pages were rate-limited)
   — used to date parks (Parque del Aire 2018-2023, Piura 2014/2024, Arequipa 2022/2024,
   Sacraca 2022, Huancayo Dec 2023, Ayacucho 2021, Pueblo Libre dismantling Jul 2019).
6. **aviationmuseum.eu** page for the museum (Castillo Rospigliosi address/hours, three
   airframes there, and a "former collection at Las Palmas, not possible to visit" list).
7. **museos.cultura.pe**, es.wikipedia (Castillo Rospigliosi, Northrop A-17, Quiñones),
   defensa.com (2013 Su-22 monument), El Comercio (2009 Parque Temático).
8. Wikimedia Commons categories were thin for Peruvian monuments (used only for 2012 Las Palmas
   serial photos by "aeroprints").

**Never used:** Grokipedia. Wikipedia survivor lists have no Peru-specific entries for the types
concerned except the A-17 article (Sacraca).

## 2. Headline findings / corrections to the received picture

- **The "Museo Aeronáutico del Perú" is two places now.** Since 23 May 2014 the public museum is
  the Castillo Rospigliosi (Calle Manuel del Pino 448, Santa Beatriz; Wed-Sun 09:00-15:00) —
  memorabilia, models, and only a handful of small airframes (Bensen gyro OB-V-1046, a Stearman,
  Canberra 204 cockpit, per aviationmuseum.eu — **not independently dated**). The airframe
  collection stayed inside **Base Aérea Las Palmas** and is not open to walk-up visitors.
- **Parque del Aire (Surco), opened 2018**, is the FAP's answer: a free public park on the base
  boundary (Jr. Belisario Suárez, -12.1537,-77.0093) holding ~16 airframes moved out of the
  museum compound: F-86F 180, T-33A 455, Hunters 634 and 142, Mirage 5DP 199, Su-22 163,
  MB-339AP 477, Y-12 339, Kuntur 401, T-37B 447, T-41D 407, T-6 450, Bell 212 660, Queen Air
  743 (as 729), an unmarked Mi-8T, and an A-37B (OSM). All but the A-37B confirmed by dated
  photos Jul 2022 / Aug 2023 / Nov 2023. This is the site a visitor should be sent to.
- **Parque Temático de la FAP, Callao (opened Jul 2009, Av. Faucett cuadra 35)** appears to have
  been dismantled/absorbed between Jul 2019 (photographed intact: An-32 363 as 392, T-37 on
  pylon, Queen Air 900, radar) and Aug 2023 (Su-22 005 "in the dump at Lima airport"; Queen Air
  900 now inside Las Palmas; the Mi-8T now at Parque del Aire). The Aug 2023 photographer
  describes the survivors (DC-6 381, DHC-5 328, Bo105 607, Bell 47 "663", C-26 341, UH-1 615,
  Mi-8 616, the T-6-in-Northrop-colours) as "preserved at Lima Airport Grupo 8 Base", and the
  2023 images show base buildings behind them. Recorded as **one restricted site, "Grupo Aéreo
  N.º 8 Heritage Display, Callao"**; the An-32 363 and T-37 "165" were not seen in 2023 and are
  left out. **Needs a human**: is any of it still viewable from Av. Faucett?
- **Su-22 identities**: the Cuartel General FAP monument (inaugurated 23 Jul 2013) is ex-027
  painted as 159; the Arequipa airport Su-22 is painted 003 but the real 003 is inside Las
  Palmas (2023 photo) — Arequipa serial left blank. Su-22 005 (ex-Parque Temático) is scrapped/
  dumped, not recorded.
- **Mirage 5P 194 is painted 182** at Las Palmas (2023). Mirage 5P4 184 photographed Mar 2024
  at Las Palmas is a stored airframe in a line of 5 (184/108/187/191/110) — storage, excluded.
- **The "Northrop 8A-3P XXXI-93-I" at Grupo 8** is, on the 2023 photograph, a T-6 Texan
  airframe in Northrop colours (T-6 canopy/cowl). Recorded as T-6, tail blank, false markings in
  aliases. The Las Palmas "NA-50 251 / XXI-41-3" is likewise believed to be a FAP full-scale
  reconstruction (no NA-50 survived); flagged in its description.
- **Canberra 240 (ex WJ757) wears 247** as the Pisco gate guard (2023). Three more Canberras
  (202, 246, 256) stand on the **Pisco seafront** — a separate public site; Canberra 251 is
  "dumped" inside the base and excluded.
- **Sacraca (Ayacucho)**: Northrop 8A-3P c/n 415 / FAP-277, force-landed 12 Jan 1957 and kept
  on the village plaza ever since; the 2022 drone video shows it cosmetically restored (silver,
  red cowling, "FAP 277") in a rebuilt plaza, locally mis-called "Douglas 277".

## 3. Judgment calls

- `model` conventions: `Su-22` + variant only when a source states it (M for 163); `Mirage 5`
  + `P`/`DP`; `Canberra` + `B(I).12`/`B.56`; `F-86`+`F`; `T-33`+`A`; `A-37`+`B`; `MB-339`+`A`/`AP`;
  `Bo 105`+`C`; `65-A80` for the Queen Air (Beech designation, alias "Queen Air; Beech 65").
- `tail_number` = true FAP 3-digit serial where a photographer has established it; painted
  false marks (159, 182, 247, 729, 003, 154, XXXI-93-I) are in aliases and description.
- F-80C "154" (Rímac) — the identity of the airframe is not established; blank, 154 alias.
- Replicas kept, flagged: Blériot XI and Ansaldo SVA.5 replicas at Las Palmas (both photographed
  2010; the Blériot is mapped on OSM outside the museum building). NA-50 251 and the Northrop-
  painted T-6 flagged as reconstruction/false identity.
- Wrecks-as-monuments: Sacraca 8A-3P (now restored) included; Puerto Maldonado An-2 237
  (derelict but standing outside the terminal 2010-2022) included with condition stated.
- `access_type`: Las Palmas, Grupo 8, Pisco gate, Talara gate, Chiclayo airport = `restricted`;
  parks/plazas/malls = `public`; Castillo Rospigliosi = `public` (ticketed, fixed hours).
- Two Hunters at Parque del Aire (634 and 142) are entered separately because both were
  photographed; if a human finds only one airframe with different marks per side, merge.
- Coordinates: Parque del Aire, Castillo, Rímac, Surco MB-339, Mall del Sur, Óvalo Retamal,
  Arequipa park, Huancayo, Ayacucho, Sacraca (village), Puerto Maldonado from OSM/Nominatim or
  aerialvisuals (airframe position where the Locator gave one); Las Palmas = the F-86 pad
  position aerialvisuals gave for the museum area; Pisco seafront = aerialvisuals site E,
  approximate; Cuartel General FAP left blank (not geocoded).
