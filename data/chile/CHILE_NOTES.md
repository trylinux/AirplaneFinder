# CHILE — research notes (all three passes: museums, base collections, monuments)

Output: `/home/claude/sa/chile/` — `cl_museums.csv` (32 sites) + one `<slug>_aircraft.csv` per site
(184 aircraft rows, 147 with tail numbers). Generated by a Python `csv` writer (UTF-8, `\n`).

## How the run went (read this first)

* The session's WebSearch budget was exhausted almost immediately (shared budget, 200/200) and every
  alternative search engine reachable by curl (Bing, DDG, Brave, Startpage, Qwant, Yandex, Mojeek, Ecosia,
  Marginalia) either served bot-junk or a captcha. Google Maps, airhistory.net, jetphotos and
  abpic search are behind Cloudflare/JS. Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons APIs rate-limited hard
  (HTTP 429) after a handful of calls. modocharlie.com (the key Chilean preservation blog, closed 2020)
  is dead but fully indexed in the Wayback Machine — that is where most of the monument leads came from.
* Consequently the **breadth of the monument pass is below what the brief expects** (Thailand/Indonesia
  scale). Every site here is backed by at least one named source, but plaza/roundabout monuments in the
  regions (Arica, Copiapó, La Serena, Talca, Temuco/Maquehue, Osorno, Balmaceda, Puerto Natales, the
  aero-club gate guards, universities) were *not* swept — see "Leads / needs a human" below.
* Files were written early and rebuilt after each increment, so an interruption loses nothing.

## Corrections / identity resolutions (with evidence)

* **MNAE Catalina "405"** — it is Canadian Vickers OA-10A c/n CV-520 = USAAF 44-34009 = CC-CGY (laahs.com
  "Manu-Tara Reborn"), so Wikipedia's 44-34009 and aerialvisuals' CV-520/CC-CGY are the same airframe.
  `tail_number` = CC-CGY (last true identity), 405 in aliases as false marking.
* **MNAE Twin Otter** — the airframe presented in 2024 as "935" is c/n 7, ex-El Tepual gate guard painted 931
  (modocharlie Dec 2019). El Tepual therefore lost its Twin Otter; only the Hunter row is kept there.
* **T-37 396** — left MNAE 23 May 2017 for the Cerrillos Metro station monument (museum post + Commons
  2017 photos). Recorded at the station, not the museum.
* **DC-3 "CC-CLDT"** — an invalid 5-letter marking; recorded under its true identity CC-CBX / 42-93390
  (LAN 214), with CC-CLDT as alias. If a human finds a data plate contradicting this, fix the tail.
* **F-80C J-333 (Chabunco)** — MNAE records give USAF 49-458; repainted natural metal c.2011.
* **Chaitén "casa avión"** — aerialvisuals' "private residence" C-47B is the crashed FACh 960 (43-16284,
  c/n 20750) turned into a house at El Amarillo; kept as a public-view site with an explicit description.
  The C-47 aerialvisuals reports painted 960 at Pudahuel therefore wears a false serial (tail left blank).
* **UP-3A Naval 402** — moved Concón → MNAE October 2015 (modocharlie); removed from the naval museum list
  and put at MNAE as `in_storage` (no dated photo of it assembled).
* **SP-2H Neptune CC-CHU** — aerialvisuals still shows it at Carriel Sur; it went to MNAE in 2013 and was
  presented restored July 2019 (tallyho). Recorded at MNAE only.
* **Twin Bonanza CC-CCM** — aerialvisuals/modocharlie had it at Coyhaique (2010); at MNAE since July 2014
  (as FACh 918). Recorded at MNAE only.
* **DC-3 CC-PJN (Melipilla)** — dismantled May 2018 and bought by Ricardo Prambs (Warbird Chile) for
  restoration at Osorno; Melipilla site dropped, Osorno listed as a lead (not a display yet).
* **SA 315B Lama** — the MNAE example (c/n 2494) is the one displayed at the Museo Histórico y Militar
  Feb 2013-2015 (ex-Ejército 161/CC-CGC per modocharlie; museum says 166). Tail 166 per museum, 161 alias.
* **A-36 vs T-36 at Alto Hospicio** — 2010 report said T-36, FACh's 2019 statement said A-36; recorded as
  A-36 with T-36 alias.
* **Beech D18S "A-104" at Torquemada** — aerialvisuals renders Naval 104 (c/n A-1027) as an Argentine
  serial; modocharlie's 2010 museum inventory confirms Naval 104.

## Judgment calls

* **Replicas** at MNAE (Blériot XI, Avro 504K, Bristol M.1C, S.E.5a, Fokker Dr.I, Voisin, Wright Flyer,
  Quiltro hang-glider) are included because the museum presents them as full-scale airframes, three of
  them airworthy builds; each description says "replica". The museum's Passarola, Langley Aerodrome,
  ornithopter and da Vinci screw models were excluded (not aircraft).
* **Nose/cockpit sections**: EB-707 Cóndor nose (MNAE, arrived July 2025) included as `under_restoration`;
  the DC-6 CC-CCC simulator cockpit (seen 1991) excluded — no evidence it is displayed.
* **Wreck-houses / derelicts**: Chaitén C-47 house and Porvenir Beech 18 included (well-known, publicly
  visible relics with identities); the Arica beach Aero Commander hulk (modocharlie 2009, unidentified,
  collapsing) excluded.
* **Motels**: three Boeing 737-200s (Concón, Gran Avenida Santiago, Quillota) and a DC-3 bought by motel
  chains in 2012-14 are recorded as public-view sites — the brief explicitly counts hotels — but all four
  lack any 2020+ confirmation.
* **Access types**: FACh/Navy/Army base displays = `restricted`; MNAE, plazas, Metro station, motels,
  Gorbea, Mejillones = `public`; technical schools, Porvenir aerodrome, Catalina at AMB, Víctor Lafón =
  `appointment`.
* **Two Bo 105s both truly serialled C-19** (Carabineros re-used the number): MNAE has the 1984 LS A-1
  (c/n S-655), the Museo de Carabineros the 1995 LS A-3 (c/n 2047). Both rows keep tail C-19 — the
  generator warns on this deliberately; the importer should treat (tail, model, museum) as the key.
* **MNAE display_status**: aircraft in the museum's remote storage compound (BAC 1-11s, Hunters 724/734,
  Mentors 147/185) are `in_storage` on the strength of GAR 2014 + aerialvisuals; C-130B 997 kept as
  `on_display` because it was photographed on the grounds in 2008 and 2012, but see open questions.
* Hunter T.72 741 at Villa Carrizo, Antofagasta, is a two-seater recorded as `trainer`.

## Deliberately blank fields

* `year_built` only where the museum or a registry gave a build/delivery date.
* Coordinates: given for MNAE (museum centre), the Metro station, and every aerialvisuals dossier site
  (airframe positions); blank for sites known only from text (Gorbea, Alto Hospicio, Villa Carrizo,
  Duhalde plaza, motels, Porvenir, Liceo Widmer, Museo de Carabineros).
* Serials blank for: the Iquique/Chucumata Hunter, the Chabunco C-45, the Rancagua Puma/C-45H/Enstrom/
  PA-12, the two 2024 MNAE UH-1Hs, the Gipsy Moth, the Mejillones Invader (identity contested), the
  Pudahuel "960" C-47, the Los Andes C-47, the El Bosque DC-3 motel, the Liceo Widmer EMB-111.
* `postal_code` blank throughout (Chilean postal codes are not used on any source).
