## NOTES

#### Sources and their weight

**1. OpenStreetMap Overpass sweep — the single highest-yield tool, exactly as briefed.**
A bounding-box query over Italy for `historic=aircraft`, `memorial=aircraft`, `memorial:type=aircraft`
and `aeroway=gate_guardian` returned **216 elements inside the Italian country area** (a wider
bbox query returned 274 including neighbouring countries). Clustered at a 600 m radius these
resolved into **117 distinct locations**, each reverse-geocoded through Nominatim. Italian mappers
tag these well: many nodes carry `manufacturer`, `model`, the unit code in `ref`, the MM serial
transcribed from the plaque in `inscription`, `start_date` for the installation, Panoramax or
Mapillary street-level image references, and `check_date` values as recent as **April 2026**.
Airframe-level coordinates from OSM are far better than anything geocoding would produce and are
used verbatim. overpass-api.de was reset by the proxy on every attempt; **overpass.kumi.systems
worked** on the second retry with a browser User-Agent and the query POSTed from a file.

**2. alatricolore.it "Velivoli sopravvissuti" (per-region pages).** The most complete Italian
compilation found: ~1000 airframe rows across 21 regional pages, giving model, MM serial (with
composite build notes — "coda di MM 6821", "muso di MM 6785") and a precise location string. It is
**undated**, has no index page (the parent `sopravvissuti.htm` 404s; only the `regioni/<region>.htm`
leaves resolve), and it colour-codes demolished airframes. Treated strictly as a **lead list**: used
for identity only where OSM, the International F-104 Society or a news item independently confirms
the site.

**3. International F-104 Society "Preserved in Italy" (i-f-s.nl).** ~120 Italian F-104s with MM
serial, unit code, town and a one-line site description. Specialist, actively maintained, and the
best single source for Italy's F-104 dispersal. Used as the second source for most F-104 monuments.

**4. storiadellefreccetricolori.it "Omaggi alle Frecce Tricolori".** A dedicated list of every
G.91 PAN and MB-339PAN placed as a monument or museum exhibit, with MM serial, town and — crucially
— **dated inaugurations** (Codroipo Feb 1998, Cesena 25 Sep 1994, Selargius 12 Sep 2004, Caselle
21 May 2006, Guidonia 7 Jun 2012, Pieve di Soligo 17 Apr 2024). This carried Friuli, Piedmont and
Sardinia.

**5. Italian local news and association pages** for current status: comune.jesolo.ve.it, voitg.net,
piavetv.net and clubfreccetricolorijesolo.org on the **2026** Jesolo AMX; leccenews24 / lecceprima /
modellismosalento on the **2022** Galatina MB-339; barlettaviva / pugliareporter / batmagazine on the
Barletta T-33; assoaeronauticafidenza.it (the owning AAA section's own site) on Fidenza; Messaggero
Veneto (2023) on the Codroipo G.91 restoration; aeronautica.difesa.it on Caraglio.

**Which compilation proved stale, and how.** alatricolore is demonstrably behind:
- It still lists the **Bovolone G.91 PAN MM 6403 at the ex-Caserma 72 Gruppo**; the Frecce Tricolori
  history site records that airframe as **relocated to Volandia in 2016**. Not recorded at Bovolone.
- It lists the **Carpi G.91 PAN MM 6264 at the aeroclub**; the same source records it transferred to
  the Fondazione Jonathan at Nervesa in **2013**. Excluded.
- It contains a large block of airframes at the **Bentivoglio scrapyard on Via Bufalotta in Rome**
  (~35 rows) flagged "Demolito" or "Venduto e trasferito / destinazione ignota". A scrap line is not
  a display under the contract's test. **All excluded** — see below.
- It knows nothing of the **2026 Jesolo AMX**, the **2024 Pieve di Soligo G.91**, the **2023 Pozzuoli
  Tornado**, the **2022 Galatina MB-339** or the **2021 Pozzuoli AMX**, all of which OSM or local
  news carry. The post-2015 AMX and Tornado hand-out wave post-dates every compilation, exactly as
  the brief predicted.

  Scuola Sottufficiali in Caserta**. `tail_number` left BLANK at Tolmezzo and the conflict stated in
  the description. One of the two is a mapper transcription error; a photograph of the Tolmezzo
  plaque would settle it.
- **Erbè (VR) F-104**: alatricolore says MM 6776, i-f-s says MM6914. Blank.
- **Leonardo Caselle F-104**: i-f-s says MM6776 — the same serial alatricolore assigns to Erbè.
  Blank at both.
- **Cancello ed Arnone F-104**: alatricolore MM 6937, i-f-s MM6772; both agree on code 9-1. Blank.
- **Caselle roundabout G.91 PAN**: Frecce site MM 6415, alatricolore MM 6420. Blank.
- **Pieve di Soligo G.91 PAN**: Frecce site MM 6249, alatricolore MM 6264. Blank.
- **Campoformido G.91 PAN**: alatricolore MM 6411, Frecce site MM 6382. Blank.
- **Politecnico di Milano MB-326**: alatricolore lists **MM 54277 twice** — at the Politecnico and at
  an Afragola monument. Blank at both.
- **Cesano Maderno**: OSM and alatricolore call it a **G.91Y**; the Frecce site calls it a **G.91 PAN
  (G.91R) MM 6453**. Recorded as G.91Y with no serial; the discrepancy is stated.
- **Cesano (Roma) Infantry School**: OSM tags it `Bell UH-1 Huey`; alatricolore identifies the machine
  as an **Agusta-Bell AB-205**. Recorded as AB-205 (the AM/EI never operated US-built UH-1s in

- **US fiscal-year numbers are not MM serials.** Rezzato T-33 (51-17484), Perdasdefogu T-33

  Italian lists. Left out of `tail_number`; recorded in the description instead.
- **`year_built` is blank on every single row.** OSM `start_date` tags on the Caravelles (1961/1962),
  the Seriate DC-3 (1935), the Roma Viscount (1948) and the Rosà Caravelle are **type-introduction
  dates**, not build dates, and were deliberately not used. The only genuine construction date found
  is Lumezzane's `construction:date=1959`, which is stated in the description but not promoted to
  `year_built` because it is a single unverified OSM tag.
- **False serials recorded as aliases**: Foligno F-104 MM 6763 wears **"MM 6850"**; Pozzuoli F-104
  MM6921 wears **"MM5122"**; Cameri Tornado MM X587 wears **"MM 5321"**; Viterbo T-6H MM 54139 wears
  **"MM 53818"**; Jesolo F-104 MM 6788 wears the false code **9-42**.
- **Composites flagged in the description, not the alias field**: Chiaravalle (tail of MM 6821 + nose
  of MM 6785), Istrana MM 6750 (nose of MM 6824), Mombarcaro (nose cone of MM 6269), Gardaland
  (tail of MM 6311), Jesolo MM 6788 (tail of MM 6797), Cavalcaselle MM 6821 (tail of MM 6807 + nose
  of MM 6922 — site not recorded, see excluded).

#### Judgment calls

- **Replica recorded and flagged**: the **Ansaldo SVA.5 at Genova Airport** is a late-1980s replica
  with an original engine, propeller and radiators, per OSM's own description. Recorded with the word
  REPLICA in the description.
- **Possible replica flagged**: the **Barletta T-33** is described in OSM as a *simulacro*. Recorded
  as a T-33 with the caveat stated.
- **Relic, not airframe**: the **B-24 at Lignano Sabbiadoro** is recovered wreckage displayed beside
  the F-104. Recorded because it is deliberately retained and publicly presented, with `ruins=yes`
  noted in the description.
- **Theme parks and commercial premises recorded as sites**: Ditellandia aqua park (Castel Volturno),
  Gardaland (Castelnuovo del Garda), Birreria Pedavena (Castello di Godego), the Gigolè shop roof
  (Chiaravalle), the Cubi srl lawn (Dossobuono), the OMA works gate (Foligno), the Cassano Magnago
  agriturismo. All meet the contract's "deliberate retention plus public presentation" test.
- **`access_type` judged by how a member of the public actually gets in**: air bases, military schools
  (Caserta, Pozzuoli, Viterbo Marescialli, Firenze, Loreto, Taranto), the Caserma Chelotti and the
  Salto di Quirra range are `restricted`; technical institutes are `appointment` (their airframes
  stand inside school grounds); roundabouts, piazzas, parks and roadside monuments are `public`.
- **Airworthy aircraft**: none recorded. The Fiat G.91 returned to airworthiness in Italy
  (vintageaviationnews) is an operating warbird, not a display.
- **`operator_country`**: `IT` throughout for Aeronautica Militare / Marina Militare / Esercito /
  Guardia di Finanza / Vigili del Fuoco airframes, including MDAP-supplied F-84Fs, F-86Ks and T-33s
  that were Italian-operated. `US` only for the **Aviano F-16 gate guardian** (USAF 31st FW) and the
  **Lignano B-24 relic**. `RU` for the Antonov An-2 at Cassano Magnago. Blank on the DC-9 5N-KAY,
  the Roma Viscount, the Seriate DC-3 and the Caselle SAGAT DC-3 where no single operator is
  established.

#### Blank fields, deliberately

- **`year_built` on all 214 rows** — see corrections above. No sourced construction/roll-out/delivery
  date was found for any Italian monument airframe in this pass.
- **`tail_number` on ~95 of 214 rows** — either no serial is published anywhere (most MB-326, G.91
  and AMX monuments wear only a unit code), or two specialist sources give different MM numbers.
  Per the brief, a monument aircraft wearing a unit code and no MM is recorded with a blank
  `tail_number` and the code in `aliases`.
- **`latitude`/`longitude` on 39 sites** — those carried by alatricolore or i-f-s but absent from
  OSM. Town-centroid geocoding would have been a guess and the contract forbids invented coordinates.
  Every OSM-derived site has airframe-level coordinates.
- **`postal_code` on a handful of sites** where Nominatim returned none.
- **`website`** on almost every site — monuments do not have them. Populated only for Fidenza (the
  owning AAA section), Gardaland and the Air Force.
- **`aircraft_name`** — populated only where a genuine name exists (Spillone for the F-104, Gina for
  the G.91, Procione for the Alitalia Caravelle, Ghibli for the AMX).
