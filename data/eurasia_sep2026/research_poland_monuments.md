## NOTES

### Sources and their weight

**The OpenStreetMap Overpass sweep carried this pass and produced most of what is above.**
Query run 10 September 2026 against `overpass.private.coffee` (both `overpass-api.de` and
`overpass.kumi.systems` failed — the former with a proxy-side connection reset on every
attempt, the latter with a dispatcher timeout). Tags queried: `historic=aircraft`,
`memorial=aircraft`, `memorial:type=aircraft`, `aeroway=gate_guardian`, and
`historic=memorial` + `aircraft:model`. A plain bounding-box query (49.0,14.1,54.9,24.2)
returned 526 elements including neighbouring countries; an `area["ISO3166-1"="PL"]` query
returned **334 elements in Poland**, which I clustered at 0.006°/0.010° into 98 candidate
sites and reverse-geocoded through Nominatim.

The brief was right: Polish mappers tag these diligently. Roughly a third of the nodes
carry a tactical number in `ref`, several carry construction numbers in `description`,
about a dozen carry `check_date` values from 2023–2025, and many link Mapillary or
fotopolska/polska-org photographs. `aeroway=gate_guardian` returned **nothing** in Poland —
Polish mappers use `historic=aircraft` universally, so anyone repeating this sweep should
not rely on the gate_guardian tag.

Second-tier sources that actually paid:
- **kielceaircollection.eu** — publishes dated per-type censuses ("Samoloty MiG-21PF w
  Polsce (obecnie)", 31 July 2025). This is the freshest systematic Polish source I found
  and it should be the first stop for a future pass. Its MiG-21PF census alone names 15
  airframes with locations and current status.
- **lotnictwo.net.pl** — the "spis statków powietrznych Wojska Polskiego" is a
  per-airframe national register with c/n, unit history and fate, and its
  "Muzea i pomniki lotnicze" photo gallery has captions of the form
  "[Pomnik] City, Type Serial". **Its TLS certificate chain is broken**: curl fails with
  "unable to get local issuer certificate" and WebFetch refuses it on the same grounds.
  I could only reach it indirectly through search-result snippets and through OSM nodes
  that deep-link individual airframe pages. **A future pass with a browser tool should
  scrape this gallery first — it is the single richest untapped source for Poland.**
- **polska-org.pl / dolny-slask.org.pl / fotopolska.eu** — object-level monument pages
  with erection dates and, crucially, a "Nie istnieje" status flag for demolished objects.
  This is how I caught the Nowa Ruda removal.
- **pl.wikipedia** — only three aircraft-monument articles exist (Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski,
  Wiechlice, Andrychów) but the Andrychów one is outstandingly detailed.
- **rosnowo.pl** — a village website with a dedicated monument page; the kind of source
  that only turns up in Polish-language search.

**Stale in a specific way:** the CKZiU Mrągowo "Pomniki lotnicze w Polsce" Leaflet map
(ckziumragowo.pl) turns out to plot only Polish Wikipedia *airmen's memorial* articles —
11 points, of which one is an aircraft. It is not a monument-aircraft compilation despite
the title. Don't spend time on it.

- **Świdnica "Fokker Dr. 3"** — recorded as Fokker Dr.I; the mapper's form kept as an alias.

  53.46–53.47N, 16.45–16.48E. Reverse geocoding puts them on "dojazd pożarowy 3, Nadarzyce"
  — firebreak roads inside the **Nadarzyce bombing range**. These are gunnery targets, not

  survey, not as a single 1B 15-027.
- **Szprotawa MiG-21F** — `operator_country` set to **RU**, not PL. It is an ex-Soviet

  Wojskowej Warszawa-Sadyba (23), Lubuskie Muzeum Wojskowe Drzonów (23), Wielkopolskie
  Muzeum Wojskowe / Cytadela Poznań (10), Muzeum Narodowe Rolnictwa Szreniawa (9),
  Muzeum im. Orła Białego Skarżysko-Kamienna (8), Muzeum Broni i Militariów Witoszów Dolny
  near Świdnica (5), and the four-airframe cluster on ul. Rzeczna in Kołobrzeg which is
  almost certainly the Muzeum Oręża Polskiego open-air park. Also excluded: Muzeum Techniki
  i Militariów w Rzeszowie (the five airframes on ul. Kochanowskiego, incl. Su-22M4 7410,
  MiG-21U 2719, An-2R SP-WNG, PZL-150 SP-KFC and a TS-11) and the four-airframe cluster on
  Al. Powstańców Warszawy in Rzeszów (MiG-23MF 460, PZL M-20 SP-TUI, PZL-150 SP_TKG and an
  AS365 Dauphin named "Marcin"), both of which read as institutional collections.
- **Replicas recorded and flagged**: PWS-26 Biała Podlaska, RWD-6 Biała Podlaska, Fokker
  Dr.I Świdnica. All three say REPLICA in the first words of the description.
- **The Warszawa BOA Tu-134A** is a counter-terrorist assault-training hull inside a police
  compound. It is intact and deliberately retained but it is not presented to the public.
  Recorded with the doubt stated; drop it if the database's display test is strict.
- **ITWL Gacek** is a towed aerial target, recorded as `drone` with `wing_type` blank.
- **Leszno SZD-43 Orion** is recorded because it is plinthed; the Aeroklub Leszczyński
  operational fleet is not.
- **`access_type` calls**: `restricted` where the airframe is inside a military or
  institutional fence (Krzesiny, Balice, Babie Doły, Fort Bema, Fort Radiowo, Warszawa
  Mokotów, BOA, Sosnowiec school, the two Wrocław school/university yards). `public` for
  everything on a street, square, park or roadside. The WZL-2 Bydgoszcz Su-22 is `public`
  because the plant explicitly invited people to come and photograph it from the road.
- **`year_built` left blank almost everywhere.** Monument dates (1969 Warka, 1975 Nowy
  Tomyśl, 1985 Szprotawa, 1994 Warszawa-Paluch, 2013 Wiechlice, 2023 Otwock) are erection
  dates and are recorded in `description`, never in `year_built`. Only Andrychów (April
  1956), Balice An-26 1403 (1972) and An-2P 1463 (1970), Fort Bema Orlik 006 (1987) and
  Otwock Iskra 1629 (1977) have sourced build dates.

### Blank fields, deliberately

- `tail_number` is blank on roughly two-thirds of the airframes. Polish monuments are
  repainted constantly and OSM mappers usually record the *type* but not the number. A
  blank here means "the surveyor did not record one", not "it has none".
- `variant` is blank on every MiG-15/Lim and most MiG-21s. Distinguishing Lim-1 from Lim-2,
  or MiG-21PF from PFM, from a monument photograph is unreliable and I would not guess.
- `year_built` blank — see Judgment calls.
- `operator_country` blank on the An-2/An-24/Yak-40 civil-looking monuments (Warszawa
  Paluch, Blizna, Rudzienice, Barcinek, Wojciechów, Brzeźnica) because no registration
  survives to say whether they were military or civil airframes.
- `latitude`/`longitude` blank for Rosnowo and Sosnowiec — I have the identity but not an
  airframe-level fix and will not geocode a village centroid into a coordinate field.
