## 3. NOTES

### Sources used and their weight
- **OpenStreetMap via Overpass (overpass.private.coffee)** — highest-value currency source in this
  pass. `historic=aircraft` across the Georgia bbox returned 74 nodes; after stripping SC, FL, AL
  and NC spill and the two reserved museums, about 20 Georgia monument nodes remained. The extract
  is live, so a node's existence is real 2025-26 evidence of a display. It gave metre-level fixes
  and, at Alpharetta, Cordele, Conyers, Cairo, Willacoochee, Blue Ridge, Brunswick, Douglas and
  Flying Cowboy, independent confirmation of Aerial Visuals entries. Main Overpass API is
  proxy-blocked; the private.coffee mirror works but times out under load — retry with backoff.
  `memorial=aircraft` and `memorial:type=aircraft` returned only South Carolina hits.
- **Aerial Visuals Locator** — the single most productive identity source, ~40 Georgia
  monument-class locations with arc-second coordinates and serials. **The URL in the brief is
  wrong**: `Locator.php` and `LocationDossier.php` live at the site ROOT, not under
  `/AirframeDossier/`. POST `Country=United States&Region=Georgia&Scope=3&ShowMap=0` to
  `https://www.aerialvisuals.ca/Locator.php`. Vintage ~2008-2015; used for identity only.
- **silverhawkauthor** (two Georgia pages, A-Fort Oglethorpe and Griffin-Woodstock) — lead list
  that added Armuchee, Hawkinsville, Blue Ridge, Warner Robins City Hall, Douglas airport T-33,
  Cordele's Titan I and A-7, and Webb Military Museum. Shares Andy Marden ancestry with Aerial
  Visuals so agreement between the two is NOT two independent sources.
- **Wikipedia survivor/display lists** — used for F-105, F-84, F-86, T-33, UH-1, AH-1, B-29, F-15,
  F-100, F-102, A-6. Gave the Alpharetta Huey, the Douglasville false-serial correction and the
  Tallapoosa park's correct name.
- **FAA bulk registry** (MASTER.txt + ACFTREF.txt, downloaded 2026-09-10) — **the registry.faa.gov
  zip 403s to a bare curl**; it needs a browser User-Agent. Once through, the Georgia owner-name
  sweep returned 203 rows but almost all are flight schools, sheriff's aviation units and drones.
  It produced NO new monument sites. It did confirm the Army Aviation Heritage Foundation fleet at
  Hampton and three ex-Army OH-58/UH-1 airframes in police service (Coweta 70-15351, Henry
  71-20592, Spalding 70-15280 / 71-20728 / UH-1V 13520) which are OPERATIONAL and excluded.
- **US Army display-helicopter loan list** — not obtainable. TACOM's Static Display Program page
  (tacom.army.mil/ilsc/donations/displays, phone 520-706-8680) states the program exists and that
  a waiting list is maintained but publishes NO downloadable roster. The Wikipedia
  "List of displayed Bell UH-1 Iroquois" and "List of displayed Bell AH-1 Cobras" pages are the
  practical substitute; for Georgia they yielded exactly one monument (Alpharetta 66-16923).
- **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location"** — NOT obtained. The archive.org id_ URL in the brief
  returns the Wayback HTML wrapper rather than a PDF, and web.archive.org repeatedly reset the
  connection through the proxy; the CDX API is blocked by egress policy. Georgia USAF civic-loan
  custody is therefore UNVERIFIED in this pass. This is the largest single gap.
- **HMdb** — near-worthless here. `results.asp?SearchFor=aircraft&State=Georgia` ignores the
  keyword and returns all 7624 Georgia markers. Georgia marks aviation history (Ben T. Epps) far
  more than it marks displayed airframes.

### County coverage
This pass swept the whole state through three state-wide instruments (the FAA bulk file,
an Overpass bbox query, and the Aerial Visuals Region=Georgia query), so no county was
skipped by design. Records landed in 33 counties: Atkinson, Bacon, Barrow, Bibb, Bleckley,
Burke, Catoosa, Chatham, Clarke, Cobb, Coffee, Crisp, Decatur, Dodge, Douglas, Fannin,
Fayette, Floyd, Fulton, Glynn, Grady, Haralson, Hart, Henry, Houston, Oglethorpe, Peach,
Pike, Pulaski (unverified), Rockdale, Seminole, Spalding, Walker. The remaining 126 counties
returned nothing from any of the three state-wide instruments; that is an absence of evidence
in the compiled sources rather than proof of absence, and per-town searching was not attempted
because at Georgia's post density it is not cost-effective. The two coverage gaps that most
likely hide real records are (a) the missing NMUSAF civic-loan roster and (b) the missing
Army static-display roster — both are custody lists that no aviation directory reproduces.
