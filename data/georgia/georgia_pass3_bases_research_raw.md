## 3. NOTES

### Sources used and their weight
1. **NMUSAF "Aircraft on Loan by Location" PDF, updated April 2016** — retrieved as a real PDF via `web.archive.org/web/20150925041956id_/` and read with pdftotext (the live URL 403s and WebFetch truncates). This produced the complete Georgia block and is the backbone of this pass. It is CUSTODY data, dated 2016, and is not evidence of 2025-26 presence.
2. **Joe Baugher USAF serial pages** — for FY1963 direct from joebaugher.com; FY1951/1952/1965/1966 via `web.archive.org/web/2023id_/`. Highest-value rung here: it is the only source that revealed the two FALSE PAINTED SERIALS at Robins.
3. **OpenStreetMap `historic=aircraft` via the overpass.private.coffee mirror** — 60 Georgia nodes/ways returned. Several carry `ref=<serial>` and give an airframe-level coordinate. Undated, but independent of the aviation-directory ancestry.
4. **Base public affairs / DVIDS** — DVIDS caption 6745162 (20 July 2021) and a Moody PA article dated 29 May 2026 are the two best currency anchors in this pass.
5. **Wikipedia type-survivor lists** (F-104, F-105, F-86, F-84, A-7) — used for corroboration only; several Georgia rows are stale (see corrections).

### Source that is DEAD — do not re-try
- **aerialvisuals.ca is gone.** Both `Airframes.php?Seeds=` and `Locator.php` now return a parked-domain 404 page served by searchvity.com; the whole site is unreachable. Google still surfaces AirframeDossier.php titles in results, and those TITLES still carry serial + c/n, which is the only residual value. Any future Georgia pass should not budget calls for it.

### Corrections with evidence
- **F-105G 63-8345 is NOT at Dobbins ARB.** Wikipedia's surviving-F-105 list (compiled "as of May 2021") still says Dobbins. Joe Baugher records it "In 2020 on display at Robins ANG, GA with the tail number of 62-4425", and the NMUSAF April 2016 loan list had already reassigned it to GA ANG (ROBINS AFB), 116 MXG/CC. Two sources beat one stale one.
- **The Robins F-105G wears a false serial.** It is painted 62-4425. The genuine 62-4425 is an F-105G at American Legion Post 325, Blissfield, Michigan (Wikipedia). Record 63-8345 as the identity.
- **The Robins F-4C wears a false serial.** True identity 63-7559; Baugher states it is "falsely marked as 68-566". A DVIDS-derived reading of the tail as "66-7566" is a misread of the same painted number. 66-7566 would be an F-4D serial, not an F-4C — a block check that independently condemns the painted number.
- **F-4C 63-7485 is NOT a 116 ACW display aircraft any more.** The 2016 loan list assigned it to 116 MXG/CC but Baugher places it "On display at Warner Robins AF Museum". It therefore belongs to the Museum of Aviation pass, not this one. EXCLUDED here.
- **Moody's air park is smaller than the 2016 loan list.** The 2016 list names ten Moody airframes; OpenStreetMap maps eight and Moody's own Facebook post says "seven aircraft". The two not found on the ground are **F-16A 79-0352** and **RF-4C 66-0467**.
  - Working hypothesis on the F-16: a DVIDS/Moody article dated 18-21 June 2024 describes the 23rd Maintenance Group completing a two-month renovation of an **F-16 static display at Freedom Park, Valdosta** — a city park, not the base. 79-0352 is very likely that airframe, still carried on Moody's loan account. **This is a hand-off to the monuments pass**, which should test 79-0352 against the Freedom Park aircraft.
  - RF-4C 66-0467 is unaccounted for. Ranked open question below.
- **Aviationmuseum.eu's Marietta table has a one-row column offset.** Its rows read "Grumman OV-1D Mohawk 54-1623", "Lockheed AC-130A 66-0186", "Republic F-84F 157452". Each identifier belongs to the row above it. Three independent anchors prove the shift: AC-130A **54-1623** and F-84F **52-6476** are both on the NMUSAF loan list under "Marietta Museum of History", and A-7E **157452** is in the Wikipedia A-7 survivor list for this museum. I have used only the three anchored serials and left the rest blank rather than propagate a de-shifted guess.

### Judgment calls
- **The 116th ACW display is recorded as one site, not two.** The wing headquarters pad sits just inside the Watson Boulevard main entrance, so the October 2021 reporting ("dedicated outside the main entrance") and the July 2021 DVIDS caption ("a pad in front of the wing headquarters") describe the same place. The OSM `note=Gate guardian` node at 32.62033/-83.59917 fits both. If a site visit shows the F-105G and F-84F sit on a separate ANG-ramp heritage row, this should be split.
- **Access type for base sites is `restricted`.** Moody's air park, the Robins 116th display, Dobbins, Savannah ANGB and the Brunswick ANG facility are all inside controlled gates and need a sponsor or an open-house/airshow day. The Brunswick F-104 is on a roadside pedestal and *may* be viewable from outside the fence; still recorded restricted because the pedestal is inside the facility boundary.
- **AHTC is recorded here and flagged.** The task said to record the Aviation History & Technology Center if no other pass had taken it. I could not check the other passes. **It is a public ticketed museum at 555 Perrin Road SE, Marietta (adults $7, Fri-Sun), not a base display, and it almost certainly belongs to the museums pass.** If the museums pass also produced it, DROP this block and keep theirs — but carry across the three anchored serials (54-1623, 52-6476, 157452) and the offset-table warning, which they may not have.
- **The Robins Nike missile** mapped in OSM at 32.59302/-83.58454 sits inside the Museum of Aviation grounds (all the OSM nodes clustered at 32.590-32.594 / -83.584 to -83.589 are museum airframes: B-52D 55-0085, C-124C, C-130E, EC-135N, RF-101C, F-89J, F-101F, WB-66D, C-141C, MiG-21, E-8C, A-10A). **Museum pass, excluded here.**

### Method note for whoever runs the next US state
The single most productive move in this pass was pulling the archived NMUSAF loan PDF and reading the whole state block at once — it yielded 30 Georgia rows including every base and ANG unit custodian, in one call. The second was Joe Baugher, which was the ONLY source that caught two false painted serials. Wikipedia's type-survivor lists were wrong or stale on two of the six Georgia rows checked.
