1. **Mighty Eighth's B-24J 44-48781 "Rupert the Roo II" IS NOT IN POOLER YET.** The
   museum's own B-24 page, read 10 Sep 2026, says Phase II of the restoration completed
   in August 2026 with the wing separated from the fuselage, component parts are away at
   off-site paint and restoration shops, and the museum "anticipates the B-24's arrival
   at the National Museum of the Mighty Eighth Air Force in early spring 2027, with
   additional preparation time required before public exhibition." It is on loan from
   NMUSAF and came from Barksdale Global Power Museum in June 2025. Under the brief's
   rule that an aircraft is recorded where it IS, this airframe gets NO line in §2. It
   should be added in 2027. (Note also that the museum's own page prints the serial as
   "44-487821", one digit too many; 44-48781 is the reading that scans.)

## 5. JUDGMENT CALLS

- **CAF Airbase Georgia and the Army Aviation Heritage Foundation are COUNTED, and every
  aircraft at both is airworthy.** The brief excludes "airworthy private warbirds merely
  based at a field". Neither of these is that. Both operate a ticketed public museum
  hangar with published visiting hours (CAF: Tue/Thu/Sat 0900-1600 at 1200 Echo Court;
  AAHF: a public museum at Atlanta Speedway Airport listed by Explore Georgia and Visit
  Henry County), and in both cases the aircraft ARE the exhibit. I have counted them and
  said so in each site's preamble. Anyone who disagrees can drop the two blocks cleanly —
  they are not entangled with anything else. Individual aircraft are away flying on
  airshow weekends; display_status on_display reflects the normal visitor's view.
- **The Douglas SBD-5, the Airbase Georgia PT-19A, T-34B and LT-6D got blank
  registrations on purpose.** The CAF registers its fleet centrally in Dallas TX and holds
  multiple examples of each of those types (two PT-19s, two T-34Bs, twenty-odd Texans). I
  can see the candidates in the registry but nothing ties a specific one to Peachtree
  City. Blank beats a guess.
- **Replicas are recorded, all flagged.** The 57th Fighter Group's P-47 and P-51, the
  Airbase Georgia B5N2 "Kate", the Delta Huff-Daland Duster, the Mighty Eighth's Bf 109
  and P-51 models, and all three Tellus spacecraft. Each says REPLICA as the first word
  of its description per the brief.
- **The National Infantry Museum's C-130 fuselage is NOT recorded.** Aerial Visuals
  dossier 2270 site C places it 8 km from the museum at 32.3328/-84.9820 and calls it
  "probably a loading trainer". It is a training aid inside Fort Moore, not a museum
  exhibit, and it belongs to the base pass if to anyone.
- **The Griffin B. Bell block is written but marked for a phone check.** These are A&P

## 9. DELIBERATELY BLANK FIELDS

`year_built` is blank on every military airframe in this file except where a source gave
a build or acceptance date (CAF P-51D 1945 from the unit's rollout date of 11 April 1945;
CAF P-63A 1944 from the acceptance date of 24 February 1944; CAF FG-1D 1943, SBD blank,
P-47N 1946 and the whole AAHF block from the FAA YEAR MFR field). No fiscal-year prefix
was ever converted into a year. Where the FAA YEAR MFR contradicted the FY serial —
UH-1H 69-15354 carries "1964" in the registry — the field was left blank rather than
pick a side.

`tail_number` is blank on the National Infantry Museum's CG-4A, UH-1H and U-6A; on the
CAF's LT-6D, PT-19A, T-34B, Kate and N2S; on the AHTC's BQM-34A and L-13; on the Mighty
Eighth's PT-17, Bf 109 and P-51; on the Delta Waco, Stolp and Huff-Daland; on both
Museum of Flight T-28s and its C-45, F-14, T-34C and BTD-1; on the Zacks T-33; and on
both 57th Fighter Group replicas. In every one of those cases a serial exists somewhere
in a directory and I could not anchor it to a second source.

`variant` is blank on the Cordele UH-1 (the FY64 13xxx block spans B and D), on the
Museum of Flight C-45 and F-14, and on the Zacks T-33.

`postal_code` is a town ZIP rather than a verified premises ZIP for Cordele, Hampton,
Dallas, Peachtree City and both Locust Grove sites.
