Site note on coordinates: fix taken from the Aerial Visuals mapped site markers for the
Eagle Building / Hangar One cluster (32 35 31.91 N 83 35 15.99 W and 32 35 28.57 N
83 35 16.01 W), not a geocoded street address. The four buildings and the air park span
roughly 32.5903–32.5939 N, -83.5896 to -83.5845 W.

Access note: the museum sits OUTSIDE the Robins AFB controlled perimeter on Heritage
Blvd; no base pass or ID check is required and admission is free. Hence `public`, not
`restricted`.

**WEBSITE WARNING (Sept 2026):** `museumofaviation.org` and `www.museumofaviation.org`
currently return a 114-byte stub that JavaScript-redirects to `/lander` — i.e. the domain
is serving a parking/lander page to direct HTTP clients. Search engines still index deep
pages, so it may be a bot/UA block rather than a lapsed domain, but every curl and
WebFetch attempt (multiple UAs, multiple paths) got the stub. **All museum-own-page
facts below were therefore taken from Wayback Machine captures of
`museumofaviation.org/portfolio/<slug>/` (captures dated 2021–2025), not the live site.**

### 3.2 Corrections made, with evidence

1. **B-1B — Aerial Visuals is wrong.** AV maps `86-0098` to the outdoor B-1 site. The aircraft is **83-0069**, confirmed independently by the NMUSAF loan register, the museum's own page, the 15 Oct 2022 log, and Wikipedia's B-1 survivor list (sixth B-1 built; to 96 BW Dyess 13 Mar 1986; to Robins Sept 2002; renamed *Midnight Train From Georgia* by April 2015).
2. **F-80C — Aerial Visuals' `54-8357` is a production-block failure.** No F-80 was ordered in FY54. The museum page, the NMUSAF loan register and the 2022 log all give **45-8357**, which sits in the FY45 P-80A block (45-8301/8720) later rebuilt to F-80C standard. Registry identity recorded.
3. **C-130E — Aerial Visuals' `64-0496` is unsupported.** Museum page, NMUSAF register and the 2022 log all give **63-7868**. Recorded as 63-7868.
4. **OV-10A — Aerial Visuals codes it `674623 USMC`.** It is USAF **67-14623**; both the NMUSAF register and the museum page say so, and the museum's own text says it went to the 504th TASG.
5. **C-45G — the museum's own page is wrong.** It prints `52-11653`, which is outside the C-45G block. NMUSAF and AV both give **51-11653**. Registry identity recorded, museum value not carried.
6. **C-47 / R4D — one airframe, two identities.** USAAF **43-49442** = USN R4D-6 **BuNo 50811**, c/n 15258. AV and the 2022 log list the Navy identity; NMUSAF and the museum list the USAF one. Recorded once, with the BuNo in aliases.
7. **U-2C — same airframe, two identity systems.** Museum/NMUSAF **56-6682**; AV records the later NASA identity **NASA 709 / N709NA**, article 349. Recorded as 56-6682.
8. **KC-97L 53-0298 — Aerial Visuals lists it twice**, once on site (site AC) and once among the aircraft *scrapped* in 2013 (site AI). The 15 Oct 2022 sighting settles it: it is present, outdoors.
9. **F-15C — 78-0479 preferred over AV's 79-0078.** The 2022 first-hand log read 78-0479 off the airframe in the outdoor restoration compound; AV lists 79-0078 unplaced. Both check against F-15C blocks; the dated sighting wins.
10. **PT-22 — three competing serials.** NMUSAF `41-1987`, AV `41-1978`, museum page `41-21039`. All three fall inside FY41 Ryan trainer blocks so no block check discriminates. NMUSAF value recorded; the others are in aliases. **Open question.**
11. **PT-19A — two airframes or one?** AV lists PT-19A **42-2809** (N54255) in the Scott Hangar *and* PT-19A **42-2802** (N51235) as unplaced. NMUSAF lists only 42-2802. The museum page gives 42-83633. The 2022 observer saw exactly one, wearing N54255. Recorded as one aircraft under 42-2802 with the alternatives in aliases. **Open question.**
12. **L-5 — three serials.** Museum `42-98184`; AV `42-98180`; NMUSAF `L-5B 44-17421 (R)`. The museum's own text admits the aircraft is *painted as* an L-5 that flew in North Africa, so the painted marking is explicitly not the identity. Recorded as 42-98184 pending resolution. **Open question.**

### 3.4 Deliberately blank fields

- **`year_built` is blank on every line.** No construction, delivery or acceptance date was sourced for any airframe, and the FAA YEAR-MFR route is closed because none of these aircraft is currently registered to the museum. Per the brief, a fiscal-year prefix is never a build year.
- **`aircraft_name`** is populated only for the B-1B (*Midnight Train From Georgia*) and the C-47 (*Francis L.*).
- **`wing_type`** is blank for all rotary_wing and missile_rocket lines, as required.
- Serial blank where genuinely unpublished: MiG-21, JN-4D, Chanute glider, Epps monoplane, P-63E, WACO CG-4A, second C-45G, F-105 simulator, and all the small AIM/AGM/AIR ordnance.

### 3.6 Judgment calls

- **`display_status` is the visitor's view.** Airframes the 2022 observer recorded as "not on public display" (F-4C 63-7477 nose, O-2A 67-21411, HH-43F 59-1578, C-45G 51-11653) are `in_storage` even though they sit inside Hangar One. The whole outdoor restoration compound and the ALC-ramp storage area are `in_storage`.
- The Scott Hangar **restoration bay is visible to visitors**, so aircraft in it are `under_restoration`, not `in_storage`.
- **Nose/cockpit sections are recorded as records** (B-29A 44-61739, C-130A 57-0478, F-15A 77-0084, F-86H 52-5751, F-4C 63-7477, WACO CG-4A). They are deliberately retained, publicly presented, real airframe structure — not derelicts. The description says "section only" in each case.
- The **F-105 simulator** is recorded as `role_type=other` because it is a presented exhibit but not an airframe; drop it if the database excludes simulators.
- **AV's "Nugteren Exhibit Hangar" = the museum's "Hangar One."** Mapped accordingly.
- Airframes that appear only in the NMUSAF register or only on Aerial Visuals with no post-2016 sighting (RB-57A 52-1475, F-86L 52-3651, F-100D 56-2928, HH-3E 67-14711/14713, H-23B 54-2928) are **kept with an explicit "presence unconfirmed" note** rather than dropped, so the next pass has somewhere to start.
