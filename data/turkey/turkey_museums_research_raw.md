## BLOCK 3 — RESEARCH NOTES

**Sources and the weight given to each.**

`spottingmode.com/wro` carried this package. Turkey has **296 locations** in it, and the per-location pages give per-airframe construction numbers, an explicit status code (`pre`/`std`/`i/a`/`dum`/`dlt`) and — critically — a dated `l/n` (last noted) field. That last column is the only currency evidence available for Turkey at all, because the two obvious photo databases are unusable from here: **airhistory.net returns Cloudflare 403 on every search endpoint**, and the two Turkish Air Force museum sites (`istanbulhavamuze.hvkk.tsk.tr`, `ankarahavamuze.hvkk.tsk.tr`) serve a JavaScript CAPTCHA wall to any fetch, so their own exhibit lists could not be read. Reached with `curl --ciphers "DEFAULT:@SECLEVEL=0"` as instructed; the location list parses out of the `<table class="wro_standard">` and the per-location airframe tables out of `<table id="main_table">`.

Second weight: **museums' own current pages** — `msomuseum.com` (whose WordPress REST API at `/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?per_page=100` returns the entire collection with full prose per airframe, and is by a wide margin the best first-party source in Turkey), and `rmk-museum.org.tr`, whose aviation collection index lives at the undocumented slug `/istanbul/koleksiyon/havacilik` (the site has no sitemap and no collection index; the slug had to be guessed).

Third: **Turkish-language sources**, which were decisive twice (see corrections). `tr.wikipedia` articles fetched as raw wikitext via `index.php?action=raw` — the MediaWiki API rate-limits aggressively and returned 429 for most category queries, but `action=raw` on individual articles works unthrottled. `goklerdeyiz.net`'s "Türkiye'de Bulunan Anı Uçaklar" series gave an independent Turkish F-104 survivor list which cross-checked ten of my Starfighter rows and is also worth handing to the monuments researcher.

Fourth: Turkish news (Anadolu Ajansı, haber.aero, Gazete Vatan, Kocaeli local press) for dated events — this is what caught the Sivrihisar Nu.D-36, the Fw 190 first flight, and the UçakPark fire.

**OpenStreetMap Overpass**: `overpass-api.de` and `overpass.kumi.systems` both failed through the agent proxy (`ws_closed_mid_exchange`, connection resets); **`overpass.private.coffee` works**. The sweep was near-worthless for discovery — OSM holds exactly **three** aviation-museum-named objects in all of Turkey (Ankara ×2 duplicated, İstanbul ×1) — but it did confirm the Ankara museum centroid and produced an incidental find: the İstanbul museum's T-37C is mapped as its own node, which is how Nominatim reverse-geocoding 40.96406/28.82554 returns "Cessna T-37C Tweetybird" as the place name.

`aviationmuseum.eu` was used as a lead list only and contributed **zero facts**; its Istanbul page 404s under the obvious slug and its İzmir naval page was not needed.

**Which compilation proved stale, specifically.** Two, in different ways.
1. **The brief's own premise about Ankara Etimesgut.** The brief expected the Etimesgut collection to be the **THK University campus**. It is not. Reverse-geocoding 39.94897/32.70255 returns "Havacılık Müzesi, Fatih Sultan Mehmet Bulvarı, Ergazi" and `tr.wikipedia` confirms this is the **Ankara Hava Kuvvetleri Müzesi**, opened 18 September 1998 by the Turkish Air Force with Sabiha Gökçen present — a 31-airframe national museum, entirely separate from THK. THK's own museum is a different, much smaller site in **Ulus, Altındağ** (Hipodrom Cad. 2, next to the 1937 parachute tower), opened 19 May 2002, six airframes.
2. **Spottingmode's own Turkey index is uneven.** Its 58-airframe İstanbul list is superb and dated to **July 2026**; its Ankara list of 31 has **no dated last-noted field on a single airframe**, and its THK list dates from a February 2015 survey. Currency inside one source varies by two orders of magnitude here and every row says which.

**Corrections made, with evidence.**
- **"Ankara – Museum" (spottingmode 7615) is the METU Science and Technology Museum**, not an air force site. Reverse geocode of 39.90156/32.77208 → "ODTÜ Bilim ve Teknoloji Müzesi, Üniversiteler Mah., Çankaya"; `tr.wikipedia` on ODTÜ BTM independently names a C-47 and an F-104 in its open-air exhibit, which matches two of the nine airframes.
- **"Eskisehir" (spottingmode 11963, 17 airframes) is the Vecihi Hürkuş Havacılık Parkı.** Reverse geocode → Uludağ Sokak, Yenibağlar Mah., Tepebaşı; Eskişehir Büyükşehir Belediyesi gives the address as Yenibağlar Mah., Uludağ Sk. No:10. It was founded 1988 as the **Eskişehir Hava Müzesi** from 1. Hava İkmal ve Bakım Komutanlığı stock, handed to Anadolu Üniversitesi by protocol on 24 September 2011, and **renamed by public vote in 2020** and transferred to Tepebaşı Belediyesi. Any directory still calling it "Eskişehir Hava Müzesi" under Air Force management is a decade out of date.
- **The Nu.D-36 replica went to Sivrihisar, not İstanbul.** The brief listed Nu.D.36 among İstanbul's replicas. Turkish press of **29 August 2026** places the flyable Nu.D-36 replica entering the M.S.Ö. inventory at Sivrihisar with a flying display. Likewise the Vecihi replica: M.S.Ö.'s **Vecihi XIV**, not a K-VI, entered inventory **28 June 2025**.
- **The Rahmi Koç F-104's year.** The museum's own English page says built in Italy 1974, so `year_built` is 1974 and the serial prefix **75** in 75-6895 is treated as an order-year prefix, not a build year. Same discipline applied throughout: **PZL P.24A serial 2015 is a serial, not a year**; F-104 62-12344, 62-711, 62-770, 62-733, 62-642 all carry FY62 order prefixes with no `year_built` written.

**Judgement calls.**

*Replicas (4 rows, all flagged, none carrying a serial).* Ankara's **Blériot "Fethi Bey"** (built 2001 for the TRT documentary *Altın Kanatlar*); Sivrihisar's **Vecihi XIV** and **Nu.D.36**. All three are recorded honestly as replicas in `description` with blank `tail_number`. The Sivrihisar **Fw 190A-5** is *not* filed as a replica — it is a rebuild from the wreck of Werknummer 1134 shot down over northern France in 1944, and is recorded with a blank tail because it carries a Werknummer and no serial.

*Repaints and false markings (8 rows).* İstanbul: DH.89A **TC-HAD** flown as TC-ERK; F-5A **14466** painted 14460; PZL P.24A **2015** painted 2145; T-34A **54-5224** wearing 54-5220's code. ODTÜ: C-47A **6066** painted 9063. Rahmi Koç: T-34A **54-5212** which carries only the code OK-12 and no serial; Vampire **G-MKVI** painted as RAF WL505; Jet Provost **G-BWDR** in RAF colours but wearing its civil registration. In every case the true identity is in `tail_number` and the marking is in `aliases`.
